### Day-by-Day Sample Week Schedules

These sample weeks are systematically built using the exact **repetition, active
production, and simulation cycles** proven to improve score outcomes [17-19].

#### Founder Schedule (10ΓÇô12 Hours/Week)
*   **Monday (1.5 Hours) ΓÇö Core Vocabulary & Collocation Focus**:
    *   *Collocation Active Drill (30 Mins)*: Study 10 new Academic Word List 
(AWL) collocations from Sublist 1 [17, 18]. Write 5 contextual sentences [18].
    *   *Speaking Part 3 PEEL Drill (30 Mins)*: Record yourself answering 3 
abstract questions [20]. Focus on using the new collocations and the **PEEL 
structure** [21, 22].
    *   *Active Retrieval (30 Mins)*: Transcribe your speaking recording; 
rewrite weak, simple vocabulary with academic alternatives [22].
*   **Tuesday (1.5 Hours) ΓÇö Writing Task 2 Coherence & Grammar**:
    *   *Paragraph Logic Drill (45 Mins)*: Write 2 body paragraphs for a Task 2 
prompt [23]. Force a strict structure: Topic Sentence $\rightarrow$ Developed 
Reasoning $\rightarrow$ Specific Example [24].
    *   *Punctuation & Syntax Drill (45 Mins)*: Rewrite 10 complex sentences 
from your draft in 3 different ways to master clause integration and avoid comma
splices [23, 25].
*   **Wednesday (1.5 Hours) ΓÇö Reading Time Management**:
    *   *Reading Passage Drill (45 Mins)*: Run a Passage 3 simulation under 
strict timing using the **15-20-25 strategy** [26, 27]. Adhere to the 
**90-second rule** to skip difficult questions [28, 29].
    *   *Error Analysis (45 Mins)*: Analyze every reading mistake [30, 31]. 
Categorize errors by cause (e.g., missed paraphrase, overthinking synonyms) and 
log them [27, 32].
*   **Thursday (1.5 Hours) ΓÇö Speaking "Brain Spark" & Intonation**:
    *   *Brain Spark Drill (45 Mins)*: Pick 5 abstract topics and generate Past,
Present, and Future "sparks" in under 3 seconds per topic [33, 34].
    *   *Intonation Recording (45 Mins)*: Record a 2-minute Part 2 monologue 
[35]. Play it back specifically to verify sentence stress and check for flat, 
syllable-timed L1 rhythm [36, 37].
*   **Friday (1.5 Hours) ΓÇö Listening Distractor Tracking**:
    *   *Listening Section Drill (45 Mins)*: Complete a Listening Section 3 or 4
practice test [38, 39].
    *   *Distractor Scan (45 Mins)*: Read the transcript while listening [40]. 
Highlight every instance where early information was corrected or retracted 
(e.g., "No, wait...", "Actually...") [41, 42].
*   **Saturday (2.5 Hours) ΓÇö Active Production Integration**:
    *   *Writing Integration (1 Hour)*: Write a full Writing Task 2 essay in 40 
minutes [43]. Spend the remaining 20 minutes self-checking using the official 
descriptors [44, 45].
    *   *AWL Vocabulary Spiral (1.5 Hours)*: Review all collocations from the 
week [17]. Complete fill-in-the-blank contextual exercises [17].

#### Intensive Schedule (20ΓÇô25 Hours/Week)
*   **Monday (3.5 Hours) ΓÇö Vocabulary & Analytical Speaking**:
    *   *Collocation & Sentence Framing (1 Hour)*: Master 15 collocations [18]. 
Write personalized examples [46].
    *   *PEEL Monologue Practice (1 Hour)*: Record 5 extended Part 3 answers 
[20]. Focus on delivering 5ΓÇô8 sentences per response [21].
    *   *Transcription & Paraphrasing (1 Hour)*: Write out your spoken responses
[22]. Force 5 paraphrased versions of your core arguments to build lexical 
flexibility [47, 48].
    *   *SRS Spaced Review (30 Mins)*: Test active recall on previous vocab 
groups [17, 49].
*   **Tuesday (3.5 Hours) ΓÇö Writing Grammar Range & Accuracy**:
    *   *Complex Sentence Drills (1 Hour)*: Practice integrating relative 
clauses, conditionals, and passive voice into sample paragraphs [36, 50].
    *   *Task Response Extension Drill (1 Hour)*: Write 3 body paragraphs, 
intentionally answering potential counter-arguments to "extend and support" your
reasoning [24, 51].
    *   *Article & Singular/Plural Check (1 Hour)*: Review past essays and 
highlight every single article (a, an, the) and plural noun [50, 52]. Ensure 
zero omissions [53].
    *   *Punctuation Audit (30 Mins)*: Run exercises specifically targeting 
sentence boundaries and comma splices [50, 54].
*   **Wednesday (3.5 Hours) ΓÇö Reading Strategic Mastery**:
    *   *Reading Passage Simulation (1 Hour)*: Complete a full 3-passage 
Academic Reading mock test [30, 55]. Adhere to the strict 15-20-25 rule [26].
    *   *Low-ROI Question Drill (1.5 Hours)*: Isolate the passage sections 
containing **Matching Headings** and **True/False/Not Given** questions [56]. 
Practice tackling these *after* you have answered the high-ROI questions [57].
    *   *Time-Sinks Diagnostic (1 Hour)*: Pinpoint exactly which questions took

*   **Thursday (3.5 Hours) ΓÇö Spontaneous Speaking & Pronunciation**:
    *   *Brain Spark Simulation (1 Hour)*: Practice mock tests with a partner or

    *   *Consonant Cluster Drilling (1 Hour)*: Practice list-reading words with 
complex consonant clusters (e.g., , , , ) [60].
    *   *Accent & Prosody Check (1 Hour)*: Record yourself reading academic 
articles [61]. Practice placing correct word stress on multi-syllable words and 
using natural pitch rises and falls [36, 37].
    *   *Speaking Self-Correction (30 Mins)*: Re-record your answers from the 
morning, forcing yourself to quickly self-correct any spoken grammar errors on 
the spot [62].
*   **Friday (3.5 Hours) ΓÇö Academic Coherence & Integration**:
    *   *Task 1 Template Writing (1 Hour)*: Practice writing Academic Task 1 
overviews using proper paragraph divisions and selective data grouping [63, 64].
    *   *CC Mapping Drill (1 Hour)*: Read model essays [65, 66]. Color-code the 
different types of cohesive devices (pronouns, demonstratives, reference chains,
and transition words) [67, 68].
    *   *Reference Chain Drafting (1.5 Hours)*: Write a 150-word paragraph on 
government transport [25]. Ensure you connect sentences using demonstratives 
("this shift", "these measures") instead of repeating the same noun [68].
*   **Saturday (4 Hours) ΓÇö Full Mock Exam & Review**:
    *   *Timed Mock Test (2 Hours)*: Sit a full Reading, Listening, and Writing 
exam under computer-delivered conditions with zero breaks [30, 69].
    *   *Comprehensive Error Audit (2 Hours)*: Self-assess your Writing and 
check your Listening/Reading answers [45, 70]. Log mistakes, adjust your weak 
areas list, and design specific drills to target those exact errors on Monday 
[27, 32].

These high-frequency systematic errors are the primary reasons why Vietnamese 
candidates plateaus at Band 6ΓÇô6.5 [108-111]. Use this list to audit your work.

#### Articles & Nouns (Writing & Speaking)
*   Γ¥î **Article Omission**: *"Sports are ( ) most powerful thing..."* [53].
    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Ensure singular, countable nouns always have an article

*   Γ¥î **Redundant/Faulty Article Addition**: *"The government should take a

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Remove articles before uncountable nouns or in fixed phrases

*   Γ¥î **Uncountable Noun Pluralization**: *"much homeworks"*, *"many

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Treat them strictly as uncountable (*"much homework"*,

#### Tenses & Verb Morphosyntax (Writing & Speaking)
*   Γ¥î **Inconsistent Tense Shifts**: Shifting between past, present, and future

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Check tense consistency. Pay close attention to tenses, as

*   Γ¥î **Verbal Suffix Omission (3SG-s)**: Omitting the *"s"* suffix for

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Audit all third-person singular verbs in the present simple

*   Γ¥î **Copula "Be" Omission**: Omitting *"be"* before adjectives (*"they ( )

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Ensure adjectives are preceded by a copula verb [112].

#### Ending Consonants & Clusters (Speaking)
*   Γ¥î **Omission of Final /s/ and /z/**: Dropping plural, possessive, or

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Intentionally pronounce ending /s/ and /z/ sounds clearly to

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Pronounce word endings with clear plosive releases [119].

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Isolate and practice clusters sequentially (*"p-l-ay"*,

#### Intonation (Speaking)
*   Γ¥î **Syllable-Timed Monotone Rhythm**: Pronouncing every word with equal

    *   ≡ƒæë **Fix**: Apply sentence stress. Highlight key content words (nouns,
