## 6) Writing — phá 6.5 → 7.0

### Task Response
- Cover **mọi nhánh** prompt (advantages/disadvantages = cân 2 body).  
- Band 7+ = **mở rộng lập luận** (claim → because → example → consequence), không liệt ý chung chung.

### Coherence (đừng robotic)
**Tránh** mỗi câu: Firstly / Moreover / Furthermore / In conclusion.  
**Dùng:**
- Reference: *This shift… / These measures…*
- Substitution: *governments → policymakers / the state*
- Topic sentence khái niệm (không chỉ linker)

**Shell Task 2 body (linh hoạt, không rote):**  
> It is frequently argued that ___. This is primarily because ___. For instance, ___. Consequently, ___.

### Task 1
- Overview **bắt buộc** Band 7: key trends, **không** nhét số vào overview.  
- 4 đoạn: Intro paraphrase → Overview → Detail A → Detail B.

### Collocation (LR)
| ❌ | ✅ |
|----|----|
| do actions | implement / take stringent measures |
| have a big role | play a crucial / pivotal role |
| make social unity | foster social cohesion |
| do research | conduct / carry out research |

### Kill-list L1 Việt (GRA)
| Lỗi | Ví dụ sai | Sửa |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| Bỏ mạo từ | Sports are best way… | Sports are **the** best way… |
| Relative / fragment | Digger dig soil can make… | A digger digs soil, **which**… |
| Verb form | used to live (sai nghĩa) | accustomed to **living** |
| Run-on / comma splice | …considerably, regions are… | …considerably; regions are… |
| Preposition transfer | associated **to** | associated **with** |

## 7) Speaking — phá 6.5 → 7.0–7.5

### Fluency
- Nói **chậm, đều** (brain faster than mouth = self-correct → FC 6).  
- Thinking phrases: *That’s a compelling question… / To be honest, I haven’t thought much about this, but…*

### Part 3 = PEEL + Brain Spark
1. **Point**  
2. **Explain** (cơ chế)  
3. **Evidence** (cụ thể)  
4. **Link** (implication / counter)

**Brain Spark ≤3s khi blank:**  
- Past (story) · Local VN (now) · What-if (conditional)

### Pronunciation (VN → PR 7)
- **Final consonants / clusters:** /s z t d/, plurals, *split ≠ spit*  
- **Stress-timed rhythm** (không flat syllable-timed)  
- Chunk thought groups; stress content words

## 8) Reading & Listening — cushion 8.0

### Reading
- **15′ / 20′ / 25′** (P1/P2/P3) — không chia đều 20′.  
- **90s rule:** không được → guess + flag.  
- Ưu tiên ROI: sentence/short/summary completion **trước**; Matching Headings + TFNG **sau** nếu kẹt time.

### Listening
- Distractor: *Actually / I changed my mind / Let me double-check*  
- Signpost: *however / consequently / on the other hand* → đoán turn answer
