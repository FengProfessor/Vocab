# 19. Epistemic Audit & Claim De-Escalation Dossier

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Status**: P0 Pedagogical Verification & Pilot Hardening (Pre-Pilot Baseline)  
> **Purpose**: Systematic audit of all theoretical, pedagogical, numerical, and technical claims in the curriculum. Every assertion is categorized into one of five strict epistemic categories to eliminate pseudo-scientific claims, arbitrary thresholds disguised as research, and false certainty before the N=1 pilot.

---

## 1. The Five Epistemic Categories

To prevent conflating empirical facts with pragmatic engineering decisions or unverified hypotheses, all curriculum specifications are classified according to this taxonomy:

1. **Category A: VERIFIED SOURCE FACT**
   - Directly verified against primary ground truth: official MOET curriculum documentation, phonological contrastive facts of Vietnamese and English, or verified system specifications of the codebase.
2. **Category B: RESEARCH-SUPPORTED PRINCIPLE**
   - Backed by established, peer-reviewed literature in cognitive science, second language acquisition (SLA), or educational psychology. The *general principle* is supported, though specific parameter values may vary.
3. **Category C: DESIGN DECISION**
   - A deliberate pragmatic engineering choice made by curriculum designers to operationalize principles within system or classroom constraints. Must NOT be presented as a universal law of nature.
4. **Category D: PILOT HYPOTHESIS**
   - An empirical assumption formulated as an testable condition for the pilot. It may turn out to be true, partially true, or false when tested on real Grade 6 learners.
5. **Category E: UNKNOWN**
   - Factors where current evidence is absent, contradictory, or heavily dependent on external individual/environmental variables that cannot be known prior to observation.

## 3. Methodological Guardrails for the N=1 Pilot

Based on this audit, the following operational constraints are strictly established:

1. **No Pseudo-Scientific Justifications**: No document or teacher instruction may claim that a parameter (e.g., 3.5s latency, 5 words, 85% passing score) is "mandated by neuroscience" or "scientifically proven." All such parameters must be documented as **Initial Pilot Parameters**.
2. **ASR Score Decoupling**: SafeHarbor ASR transcript matching is completely decoupled from pedagogical progress gates. An ASR failure must never prevent a child from completing a lesson or receiving their achievement badge.
3. **Observational Honesty**: If an empirical observation contradicts our initial parameters (e.g., if 5 new words is too many for a highly anxious learner), the parameter must be adjusted downward immediately without declaring the learner "failing."
