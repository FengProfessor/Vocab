# 03. Diagnostic Assessment System: Low-Stakes Adaptive Intake

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Authors**: Assessment Specialist (Role I), Child Motivation / Anxiety Specialist (Role E), Synthesis Lead (Role L)  
> **Diagnostic Philosophy**: **FIND THE STARTING POINT, DO NOT PRODUCE SHAME.** A diagnostic test must never yield a red "FAIL" or a demoralizing 2/10 score. Its sole function is to discover where the learner's emotional and linguistic footing is solid.

---

## 1. Diagnostic Architecture Overview

- **Duration**: 12–18 minutes total.
- **Delivery Mode**: Hybrid (Digital interactive micro-tasks on LingoPro OR 1-on-1 teacher interview checklist).
- **Format**: Dynamic branching with 3 stages (Warm-up $\rightarrow$ Core Ladder $\rightarrow$ Transfer Probe).
- **Early-Stop Mechanism**: If a learner fails 2 consecutive items within any skill branch, that branch immediately terminates with a warm, encouraging transition message, defaulting to the baseline safety level.

```
                    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                    │      STAGE 1: AFFECTIVE & RECEPTIVE WARMUP   │
                    │      (3 minutes, 100% success-guaranteed)    │
                    └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                           │
                                           ▼
                    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                    │      STAGE 2: ADAPTIVE 8-BRANCH LADDER       │
                    │      - Branch A: Listening Comprehension    │
                    │      - Branch B: Phonemic Awareness          │
                    │      - Branch C: Vocabulary Receptive/Recall │
                    │      - Branch D: Syntax & Sentence Assembly  │
                    └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                           │
                                           ▼
                    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                    │      STAGE 3: COMFORTABLE EXPRESSION PROBE   │
                    │      (Optional Speaking Reflex, SafeHarbor) │
                    └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                           │
                                           ▼
                    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                    │   OUTPUT: BẢN ĐỒ NĂNG LỰC (LINGO POWER MAP)   │
                    │   (No percentage marks; custom starting unit)│
                    └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. The 11 Skill Facets Evaluated

| Facet ID | Skill Dimension | Method of Assessment | What Success Looks Like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-01** | **Affective Readiness & Confidence** | Self-selected 3-point visual emoji scale (*Lo lắng / Bình thường / Tự tin*) | Learner feels emotionally safe to interact. |
| **F-02** | **Listening: Word Recognition** | Hear slow audio (`book`, `milk`, `run`) $\rightarrow$ tap 1 of 3 pictures | Identifies familiar acoustic word shapes. |
| **F-03** | **Listening: Action Instructions** | Hear *"Stand up"*, *"Open your book"* with visual clue $\rightarrow$ tap matching gif | Comprehends basic classroom imperatives. |
| **F-04** | **Phonemic: Final Consonant Hearing** | Hear minimal pair (*cat* vs *cap*, *ship* vs *sheep*) $\rightarrow$ select which was said | Discerning acoustic boundaries in coda/vowel. |
| **F-05** | **Vocabulary: Receptive Recognition** | Given image of school backpack $\rightarrow$ select word `bag` among 3 choices | Receptive lexicon access without spelling stress. |
| **F-06** | **Vocabulary: Productive Recall** | Given image of soccer ball $\rightarrow$ Word Bank chips $\rightarrow$ assemble `football` | Retrieval of core nouns from visual prompt. |
| **F-07** | **Syntax: S-V-O Word Order** | Scrambled chips: `[pizza] [I] [like]` $\rightarrow$ tap order $\rightarrow$ `I like pizza` | Distinguishes Subject-Verb-Object word order. |
| **F-08** | **Reading: Sentence Meaning** | Read: *"I have two cats."* $\rightarrow$ tap matching picture (2 cats vs 1 dog) | Extracts simple proposition without translating. |
| **F-09** | **Writing: Slot Completion** | *"My name is _____."* $\rightarrow$ type or select own name | High-comfort identity production. |
| **F-10** | **Speaking: Repetition & Intelligibility**| SafeHarbor private recording: *"I like games."* | Produces audible final /s/ with $\ge 70\%$ match. |
| **F-11** | **Survival Interaction** | Screen displays: *"What do you say when you don't understand?"* $\rightarrow$ chooses *"Can you help me?"* | Uses functional classroom survival language. |

## 3. Step-by-Step Diagnostic Protocol (12 Items)

### Item D-01: Emotional Icebreaker (Affective State)
- **Prompt (in Vietnamese)**: *"Chào bạn! Hôm nay bạn cảm thấy thế nào khi gặp bài tiếng Anh này?"*
- **Choices**:
  - `[😊]` Hào hứng thử sức
  - `[😐]` Hơi lo nhưng sẵn sàng
  - `[😰]` Rất sợ và ngại
- **Pedagogical Action**: If `[😰]`, system automatically enables **Soft Support Mode** (extended audio playback, zero countdown timers, 100% bilingual tooltips).

### Item D-02: Auditory Comprehension (Receptive Word)
- **Audio Prompt**: Audio plays clearly at 0.8x: *"A blue backpack"*
- **Options (Images)**:
  - Option A: Blue backpack (Correct)
  - Option B: Red pencil
  - Option C: Yellow bicycle
- **Scoring**: Correct $\rightarrow$ proceed to D-03. Incorrect $\rightarrow$ flag F-02 as Needs Foundation.

### Item D-03: Auditory Classroom Imperative
- **Audio Prompt**: *"Please open your book."*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: Icon of opening a book (Correct)
  - Option B: Icon of sleeping
  - Option C: Icon of eating an apple

### Item D-04: Acoustic Minimal Pair Discrimination
- **Audio Prompt**: Plays audio: *"seat"*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: `seat` (ngồi) (Correct)
  - Option B: `sit` (ngồi xuống)
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Detects whether student can distinguish /iː/ vs /ɪ/.

### Item D-05: Final Consonant Ear Check
- **Audio Prompt**: Plays audio: *"books"* (with clear final /s/)
- **Prompt**: *"Bạn nghe thấy âm gió xì hơi /s/ ở cuối không?"*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: Có âm /s/ (Correct)
  - Option B: Không có âm /s/

### Item D-06: High-Utility Verb Meaning
- **Prompt**: Từ nào có nghĩa là **"thích"**?
- **Options**:
  - Option A: `like` (Correct)
  - Option B: `look`
  - Option C: `live`

### Item D-07: S-V-O Sentence Builder
- **Prompt**: *"Hãy chạm vào các từ để ghép thành câu: 'Tôi chơi bóng đá'."*
- **Word Chips**: `[football]` `[play]` `[I]`
- **Correct Target**: `I play football.`
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Detects if student tries to arrange `I football play` (L1 interference) or successfully applies English S-V-O syntax.

### Item D-08: Negative Sentence Pattern
- **Prompt**: *"Tôi không thích cá."*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: `I don't like fish.` (Correct)
  - Option B: `I no like fish.`
  - Option C: `I not fish.`

### Item D-09: Receptive Reading Proposition
- **Text displayed**: *"My friend is 12 years old. His name is Nam."*
- **Question**: *"Bạn của tôi tên là gì và bao nhiêu tuổi?"*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: Nam, 12 tuổi (Correct)
  - Option B: Nam, 10 tuổi
  - Option C: Minh, 12 tuổi

### Item D-10: Classroom Survival Strategy
- **Prompt**: *"Khi thầy cô nói tiếng Anh quá nhanh và bạn không nghe kịp, bạn sẽ nói câu nào?"*
- **Options**:
  - Option A: `Please speak slowly.` (Làm ơn nói chậm lại ạ) (Correct)
  - Option B: `I am very good.`
  - Option C: `Goodbye teacher.`

### Item D-11: Written Formulaic Slot
- **Prompt**: Hoàn thành câu giới thiệu bản thân:
- **Text**: `I _____ in Grade 6.`
- **Options**:
  - Option A: `am` (Correct)
  - Option B: `do`
  - Option C: `have`

### Item D-12: Optional Voice Echo (SafeHarbor)
- **Prompt**: *"Hãy nghe câu mẫu và bấm mic nói lại: 'This is my bag.'"*
- **Audio Model**: Provided at 0.8x.
- **Evaluation**: Non-punitive. Evaluates whether final /s/ or /g/ is attempted. Score $\ge 60\%$ marks speaking readiness. If student clicks "Bỏ qua" (Skip), system notes: *Speaking anxiety present; prioritize silent listening buffer first.*

```
👉 Chặng 0: "Tiếng Anh là chuyện nhỏ" (Stage 0: English Is Safe)

```

## 5. Placement Decision Matrix

| Diagnostic Result Pattern | Placed Stage & Lesson | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Fails D-02, D-05, D-07, skips D-12 | **Stage 0: Lesson 001** | Severe anxiety, complete lack of auditory and syntax foundation. Must begin at Ground Zero. |
| Passes D-02, D-06, fails D-07, D-08 (Syntax Blindness) | **Stage 0: Lesson 003** | Has receptive vocabulary but cannot construct a sentence. Skip greetings, dive into high-utility chunks. |
| Passes D-02 to D-08, struggles with D-04, D-05 (Acoustic/Phonics) | **Stage 1: Lesson 007** | Understands simple sentences but lacks phonetic accuracy and confidence in spoken interaction. |
| Passes $\ge 11/12$ items with high confidence | **Stage 2 / Fast-Track Bridge** | Has functional Pre-A1; ready for accelerated progression toward Grade 6 school curriculum. |
