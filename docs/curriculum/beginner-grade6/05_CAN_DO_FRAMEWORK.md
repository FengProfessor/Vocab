# 05. Can-Do Competency Framework: The Communicative Spine

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Authors**: Curriculum Architect (Role A), Assessment Specialist (Role I), Synthesis Lead (Role L)  
> **Guiding Principle**: **DO NOT MEASURE PROGRESS BY WORD COUNT ("Đã học 700 từ"). MEASURE PROGRESS BY WHAT THE CHILD CAN DO IN ENGLISH.**

---

## 1. Can-Do Architecture Overview

Every learning unit in the LingoPro Foundation Curriculum is defined by a primary communicative **Can-Do Statement**. Grammar rules and vocabulary items are strictly subordinate to the Can-Do objective.

Each Can-Do statement is mapped across 8 distinct dimensions:
1. **Communicative Purpose**: What practical human interaction is achieved?
2. **Core Lexicon**: 5–8 high-utility words.
3. **Formulaic Chunks**: 2–4 reusable frames.
4. **Grammar Support**: Inductive structural pattern.
5. **Pronunciation Focus**: Specific phonetic target for Vietnamese learners.
6. **Listening Check**: Receptive verification.
7. **Speaking Check**: Productive oral verification via SafeHarbor.
8. **Mastery Verification**: Exact evidence threshold required to certify completion.
