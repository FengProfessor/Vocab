## 1. Coding Key & Two-Dimensional Evaluation

### Dimension A: Learning Performance Scale
- **[ I ] — Independent**: Produces target word/chunk accurately without an immediate auditory model.
- **[ M ] — Modelled**: Produces target immediately after echoing the teacher or audio model.
- **[ P ] — Prompted**: Requires gestural cue, visual card, or binary choice ("A hay B?") to produce target.
- **[ NY ] — Not Yet**: Cannot produce target despite two scaffolds; remains silent or provides an unrelated response.

### Dimension B: Learner Experience Self-Report
*(Ask the learner directly after key tasks or at session end)*:
- **🙂 Dễ / ổn**: "Con thấy bài này vừa sức, thoải mái."
- **😐 Hơi khó**: "Con thấy hơi khó một chút nhưng vẫn làm được."
- **🙁 Khó**: "Con thấy bài này khó quá, bị rối."
- **🛑 Con muốn dừng**: Immediate session pause condition.

### Multidimensional Pronunciation Tracking (Human Observation):
- **Intelligibility**: Comprehensible (C) / Context-Dependent (CD) / Unclear (U).
- **Target Feature Attempt**: Attempted (A) / Omitted (O) / Hypercorrected (H). *(No exaggerated stops required)*.
- **Fluency**: Continuous (CT) / Segmented (SG) / Broken (BK).

## 5. Post-Lesson Synthesis Decision

Based on the evidence pattern across the 3 Can-Dos above:

- `[ ] ADVANCE`: Core Can-Dos achieved Independently or Modelled; Self-Report 🙂/😐. $\rightarrow$ *Proceed to Lesson 002.*

- `[ ] REPEAT PART`: Confusion in asking question; Self-Report 🙁. $\rightarrow$ *Replay ball roll game before Lesson 002.*
- `[ ] SIMPLIFY`: Multiple Can-Dos "Not Yet"; Self-Report 🙁/🛑. $\rightarrow$ *Focus only on Name & Age; omit question; increase visual scaffolding.*
