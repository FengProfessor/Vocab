# 14. Content Style Guide & Child-Safety Protection Protocol

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Authors**: Young Learner ESL Specialist (Role B), Child Anxiety Specialist (Role E), Synthesis Lead (Role L)  
> **Core Mandate**: **CONTENT MUST BE AGE-APPROPRIATE FOR 11–12-YEAR-OLDS, RESPECTFUL OF THEIR COGNITIVE MATURITY, 100% ETHICALLY SAFE FOR MINORS, AND COMMUNICATIVELY AUTHENTIC.**

---

## 1. The 7 Content Quality Filters

Every single sentence, example, and audio prompt in LingoPro must pass all 7 Quality Filters before deployment:

| Filter # | Quality Criterion | Passing Standard | Failing Example (REJECTED) | Approved Replacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **QF-1** | **Grammatically Sound** | Standard international English grammar. | *"He like pizza."* (unscaffolded error) | *"He likes pizza."* |
| **QF-2** | **Authentic & Natural** | Used by native speakers in real everyday life; not robotic "textbook English". | *"Is this a pencil? Yes, it is a pencil."* | *"What's this? - It's my pen."* |
| **QF-3** | **Age-Appropriate (10–14yo)** | Resonates with secondary school life, games, sports, friends, and family. | *"I must submit the quarterly budget."* (Adult) | *"I must finish my math homework."* |
| **QF-4** | **Anti-Babyish Dignity** | Respects 6th-grade cognitive maturity; avoids preschool condescension. | *"See the little fluffy doggy!"* (Toddler) | *"I have a cute dog. His name is Lucky."* |
| **QF-5** | **Beginner-Friendly Syntax** | Clear S-V-O syntax without convoluted subordinate clauses. | *"Although it was raining, we proceeded."* | *"It's raining, but we can play inside."* |
| **QF-6** | **High Utility & Generative** | Can be reused across multiple life contexts by substituting slots. | *"The platypus lays eggs."* (Static fact) | *"I like playing badminton with my friend."* |
| **QF-7** | **Self-Explanatory & Clear** | Meaning is transparent from context and picture without a paragraph of meta-explanation. | Abstract idiom: *"It's raining cats and dogs."* | Clear descriptive: *"It's rainy today. Take an umbrella."* |

## 2. Child Safety & Minor Protection Standards

This curriculum is designed specifically for children aged 10–14. The following strict safety bans are programmatically and pedagogically enforced:

### Strictly Prohibited Content Domains:
1. **NO Violence or Graphic Themes**: Zero depictions of weapons, physical abuse, self-harm, or graphic horror.
2. **NO Alcohol, Tobacco, or Substance Reference**: Zero references to beer, wine, smoking, vaping, or pharmaceuticals.
3. **NO Gambling or High-Risk Financial Speculation**: Zero references to betting, lottery, crypto, loans, or financial debt.
4. **NO Adult Workplace or Bureaucracy Scenarios**: No business contracts, corporate promotions, or workplace politics.
5. **NO Dating, Romance, or Mature Relationships**: Scenarios focus strictly on friendship, peer camaraderie, family, and mentorship.
6. **NO Dangerous Stunts or Challenges**: No content encouraging reckless physical behavior or dangerous internet challenges.

### Privacy & Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Protection:
- Lessons never require students to disclose sensitive private information (home address, parental income, phone numbers).
- **The Fictional Freedom Rule**: Whenever a personalization prompt appears (e.g. *"What pets do you have?"* or *"Where do you live?"*), students are explicitly informed that **imaginative or fictional answers are 100% welcomed and praised**:
  - *"I live in a castle."* (Accepted and praised for correct structure!)
  - *"I have three pet dinosaurs."* (Accepted and praised!)

## 3. Pedagogical Tone & Voice Guidelines

- **Empathetic & Warm**: The narrator/teacher tone is that of an encouraging, cool older sibling or trusted coach, not a stern disciplinarian.
- **Vietnamese Cultural Grounding**: Contexts feature realistic Vietnamese life (bicycles, school uniforms, badminton in the schoolyard, Tet holiday, pho, banh mi, family meals) alongside international cultural curiosity.
- **Praise Effort and Specificity**:
  - ❌ *"Good job!"* (Too generic).
  - ✅ *"Tuyệt vời! Con phát âm đuôi /s/ của từ 'books' nghe rất rõ và tự nhiên!"* (Specific, informative praise).
