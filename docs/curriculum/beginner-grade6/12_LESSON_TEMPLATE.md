## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Correction Strategy
| Common Error | Linguistic Reason | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| [e.g. "I no like"] | L1 transfer from Vietnamese "Tôi không thích" | "Gần đúng rồi con! Nhớ khối siêu nhân 'don't like' nhé: 'I don't like fish'!" |
| [e.g. Swallowing /k/] | L1 unreleased final stops | "Con nhớ gõ nhẹ một tiếng trong họng nào: /k/, /laɪk/!" |

---

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway (If student struggles)**:
  - Reduce choices from 4 to 2 (binary option).
  - Provide audio model immediately before speaking.
  - Allow pointing or touching word-chips instead of unassisted recall.
- **High-Flyer Pathway (If student succeeds quickly)**:
  - Add 2 optional vocabulary items to their Word Bank.
  - Prompt an extension question: "Why do you like it?"
  - Invite student to act as "Assistant Coach" to help a peer.

---

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (FSRS-Aligned)

- **Minute 4**: Record 1 sentence into SafeHarbor microphone.
- **Minute 5**: Share sentence with mom/dad/friend.
- **Parent Support Tip**: *"Bố mẹ chỉ cần lắng nghe và khen ngợi con nói to, rõ ràng, tuyệt đối không bắt lỗi ngữ pháp của con."*
