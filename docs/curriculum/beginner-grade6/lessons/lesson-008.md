# Lesson 008: My Favorite Outfit (Trang phục yêu thích & Màu sắc)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-008`
- **Stage**: `Stage 1: Me & My World`
- **Thematic Domain**: Colors, Clothing & Personal Style
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001, 002, 005, 007
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Describing Clothing & Colors)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can state my favorite color and describe what I wear using 'Color + Clothes' word order."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin nói về màu sắc yêu thích và trang phục mình mặc với trật tự từ chuẩn xác bằng tiếng Anh!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `blue` /bluː/, `black` /blæk/, `white` /waɪt/, `red` /red/, `yellow` /ˈjeloʊ/, `shirt` /ʃɜːrt/, `shoes` /ʃuːz/, `wear` /wer/, `jacket` /ˈdʒækɪt/, `color` /ˈkʌlər/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 7 items)**: `blue`, `black`, `white`, `red`, `shirt`, `shoes`, `wear`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `My favorite color is [Color].` (/maɪ ˈfeɪvərɪt ˈkʌlər ɪz.../)
- `I wear [a Color Shirt].` (/aɪ wer ə.../)
- `I wear [Color Shoes].` (/aɪ wer.../)
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Attributive Adjective Position: `[Color Adjective] + [Noun]` (e.g. *blue shirt*, NOT *shirt blue*).
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Ngược với tiếng Việt nói 'áo xanh' (đồ vật trước, màu sau), trong tiếng Anh màu sắc luôn đứng trước làm vệ sĩ cho đồ vật: 'a blue shirt', 'black shoes'!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Initial /ʃ/ in *shirt / shoes* and final /z/ in *shoes*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - *Initial /ʃ/*: "Đặt ngón tay lên môi làm dấu 'suỵt': sh... shirt, shoes!"
  - *Plural /z/*: "Cổ họng rung như tiếng chú ong vo ve ở cuối từ shoes: /ʃuːz/".

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: Colored swatches (Blue, Black, White, Red), Realia props: A white school uniform shirt, black shoes, blue cap.
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Avatar Wardrobe L-008.

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & Color Spotting (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "The Color Touch Game" (Chạm vào màu sắc).
- Teacher: "Touch something blue in this room!" $\rightarrow$ Students touch blue pens or notebooks.
- Teacher: "Touch something black!" $\rightarrow$ Students touch their backpacks or shoes.
- (Khởi động vui vẻ, kích hoạt năng lượng)

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Feeling Check (Lesson 007 callback).
- Teacher points to 3 students: "How are you today?"
- Students: "I'm happy today!" / "I'm tired today!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Avatar Wardrobe (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher dresses a digital avatar on screen.
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào màu xanh dương) "My favorite color is blue!"
  - *Teacher*: (Kéo áo sơ mi trắng vào nhân vật) "Today I wear a white shirt."
  - *Teacher*: (Kéo đôi giày đen vào nhân vật) "And I wear black shoes."
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào toàn bộ bộ đồ) "Look: a white shirt, black shoes! Very cool!"
- **Comprehension Check**: "Áo màu gì? Giày màu gì?" $\rightarrow$ "Áo trắng, giày đen ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Ear-Check & Articulatory Practice (Minutes 18–25)
- **Minimal Pair Check**:
  - Token A: *sơt* (âm /s/ mỏng) vs Token B: *shirt* (âm /ʃ/ dày uốn môi).
  - "Con nghe thấy âm nào tròn môi làm dấu suỵt? B là đúng!"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - "Cả lớp làm dấu suỵt nào: sh... shirt! sh... shoes!"
  - "Đừng quên tiếng ong vo ve ở cuối từ shoes: /ʃuːz/!"

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder & Syntax Reversal Guard (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Word Order Chip Challenge on LingoPro:
  - Yellow Color Chip: `[blue]` `[white]` `[black]` `[red]`
  - Green Clothes Chip: `[shirt]` `[shoes]` `[jacket]`
  - Drag into frame: `[I wear]` + `[a]` + `[Color]` + `[Clothes]`
- **The "Syntax Guard" Alert**: If a student drags `[shirt]` before `[blue]`, the app plays a funny buzzer and flips the chips into the correct order: `[blue]` + `[shirt]` with a friendly tip: *"Màu sắc đi trước đồ vật con nhé!"*.

### Phase 6: Junior Fashion Runway Game (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Fashion Interview.
- Student A looks at Student B's outfit:
  - *Student A*: "What is your favorite color?"
  - *Student B*: "My favorite color is black! Today I wear a white shirt."
  - *Student A*: "Cool! I wear blue shoes!"
- Switch roles. Every pair practices describing their real school uniform.

### Phase 7: Exit Can-Do Ticket & Style Badge (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Each student stands up, points to their outfit, and delivers their style declaration:
- **Exit Statement**:
  - *"Today I can say: 'My favorite color is blue. I wear a white shirt.'!"*
- Teacher awards the "Fashion Designer" badge.

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| *"I wear a shirt white"* | L1 word order transfer: *áo trắng*. | "Đổi chỗ hai từ lại nào con ơi! Màu sắc là người vệ sĩ đi trước bảo vệ đồ vật: 'a white shirt'! Nói lại nghe rất chuẩn!" |
| Pronouncing *shoes* as *su* | L1 unreleased vowel and zero coda /z/. | "Làm dấu suỵt tròn môi và rung đuôi nào con: /ʃuːz/! Rất sang trọng!" |
| Saying *"I wear shoe"* | Forgetting plural for paired items. | "Giày có 2 chiếc đi theo đôi, nhớ thêm đuôi ong vo ve nhé: 'shoes'!" |

---

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Provide color-coded word cards directly on desk. Student aligns: `[Blue] [Shirt]` and echoes: "I wear a blue shirt."
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Add a contrasting favorite: *"My favorite color is red, but today I wear a black shirt."*

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (LingoPro App)
