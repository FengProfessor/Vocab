# Lesson 010: My Secret Space (Căn phòng nhỏ của con & There is/are)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-010`
- **Stage**: `Stage 1: Me & My World`
- **Thematic Domain**: Bedroom, Personal Space & Furniture
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001, 005, 006, 008, 009
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Describing Rooms & Spaces)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can describe my bedroom using 'There is a...' for 1 item and 'There are [number]...' for multiple items."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin mô tả căn phòng ngủ của mình bằng mẫu câu 'There is a...' (có 1 món) và 'There are...' (có nhiều món) mà không bị nhầm lẫn!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `bed` /bed/, `desk` /desk/, `chair` /tʃer/, `room` /ruːm/, `bedroom` /ˈbedruːm/, `there` /ðer/, `big` /bɪɡ/, `small` /smɔːl/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 6 items)**: `bed`, `desk`, `chair`, `room`, `there`, `big`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `There is a [Singular Noun].` (/ðer ɪz ə.../)
- `There are [Two / Three Plural Nouns].` (/ðer ɑːr.../)
- `My room is [big / small].` (/maɪ ruːm ɪz.../)
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Existential Quantifier:
  - 1 item: `[There is] + [a] + [Noun]`
  - $\ge 2$ items: `[There are] + [Number] + [Noun + s]`
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Trong tiếng Việt ta nói 'Có một cái giường'. Nhưng trong tiếng Anh KHÔNG dùng từ 'Have', mà dùng cặp bài trùng: 'There is' (khi có 1) và 'There are' (khi có từ 2 trở lên)!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Voiced dental /ð/ in *there* and affricate /tʃ/ in *chair*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - *There*: "Cắn nhẹ đầu lưỡi và rung: th... there /ðer/".
  - *Chair*: "Bật mạnh luồng hơi như tiếng chắt lưỡi: ch... chair /tʃer/".

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: A dollhouse room box or 3D furniture paper cutouts (*Bed, Desk, 2 Chairs*).
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Room Architect L-010.

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & Room Peek (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "The Key to My Room" (Chìa khóa mở cửa phòng).
- Teacher makes a funny key-turning sound: *Click click click!*
- Teacher: "Chào cả lớp! Hãy tưởng tượng con vừa đi học về và mở cánh cửa phòng ngủ riêng của mình ra. Căn phòng của con trông như thế nào?"
- Học sinh hào hứng chia sẻ về căn phòng mơ ước của mình.

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Best Friend Flash (Lesson 009 callback).
- Teacher: "Who is your friend?" $\rightarrow$ Student: "This is my friend, Nam. He is funny!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Dream Bedroom (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher projects a cozy, modern bedroom illustration on screen.
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào toàn bộ căn phòng) "Welcome to my room! My room is small, but very cozy."
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào chiếc giường êm ái) "Look! There is a bed."
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào chiếc bàn học gỗ) "There is a desk."
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào 2 chiếc ghế tựa xoay) "And look here: one, two! There are two chairs! Listen to the ending: chairs!"
- **Comprehension Check**: "Có mấy cái giường? Dùng There is hay There are? Có mấy cái ghế? Dùng There is hay There are?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "1 cái giường dùng There is; 2 cái ghế dùng There are ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Discrimination & The Singular/Plural Switch (Minutes 18–25)
- **Acoustic Ear-Check**:
  - Teacher plays: Token A: `There is a chair` vs Token B: `There are two chairs`.
  - "Con nghe thấy âm nào có số nhiều xì hơi? B là 2 cái ghế!"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - Luyện âm /ð/ rung lưỡi: `there... There is!`
  - Luyện âm /tʃ/ trong từ *chair*: `ch... chair... two chairs!`

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Room Architect Puzzle on LingoPro:
  - Blue Existential Slot: `[There is a]` (Singular) / `[There are]` (Plural)
  - Yellow Furniture Slot: `[bed]` `[desk]` `[chair]` `[two chairs]` `[three books]`
- Students take turns building room inventory:
  - 1 bed $\rightarrow$ `[There is a]` + `[bed]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"There is a bed."*
  - 2 chairs $\rightarrow$ `[There are]` + `[two chairs]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"There are two chairs."*

### Phase 6: Junior Architect Floorplan Game (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Room Description.
- Every student receives a blank room grid and places 3 furniture stickers (e.g. 1 bed, 1 desk, 2 chairs).
- Student A shows their floorplan to Student B:
  - *Student A*: "My room is big! There is a bed and there is a desk. There are two chairs!"
  - *Student B*: "Nice! My room has one bed and two desks!"
- Switch roles. Every pair practices 3 room exchanges.

### Phase 7: Exit Can-Do Ticket & Architect Badge (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Each student steps up and describes their room in 2 sentences:
- **Exit Statement**:
  - *"Today I can say: 'My room is small. There is a bed. There are two chairs.'!"*
- Teacher awards the "Junior Architect" badge.

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| *"Have a bed in my room"* | L1 literal transfer: *Có một cái giường* (Dùng chữ have). | "Nhớ quy tắc nhé con: 'Have' chỉ dùng khi con sở hữu cái gì đó ('I have a pen'). Khi nói 'ở đó có...', tiếng Anh dùng 'There is a bed'!" |
| *"There is two chairs"* | Agreement mismatch. | "Có 2 chiếc ghế số nhiều rồi, đổi sang chiếc chìa khóa số nhiều 'There are' nào con: 'There are two chairs'!" |
| Dropping /ð/: Pronouncing *đeo* thay vì *there* | L1 lack of dental /ð/. | "Cắn nhẹ đầu lưỡi và rung mô-tơ nào: th... there! Rất đẹp!" |

---

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Provide 2 visual picture cards (1 bed vs 2 chairs). Student points and echoes: "There is a bed. There are two chairs."
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Add a color adjective: *"There is a white bed. There are two blue chairs."*

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (LingoPro App)
- **Minute 1–2**: Open LingoPro $\rightarrow$ Lesson 010 $\rightarrow$ Listen to 3 room descriptions.
- **Minute 3**: Drag word chips to match 2 room illustrations (`There is` vs `There are`).
- **Minute 4**: Tap microphone $\rightarrow$ Look around your real bedroom and record: *"There is a bed in my room."*
- **Minute 5**: Show your bedroom to parents and speak the sentence.

## 8. Mastery Passing Gate
- **Existential Agreement**: $100\%$ zero-error distinction between `There is a [singular]` and `There are [plural]`.
- **Speech Clarity**: Clear /ð/ on *there* with $\ge 70\%$ SafeHarbor match.
