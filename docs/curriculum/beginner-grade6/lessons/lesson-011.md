# Lesson 011: Animal Friends (Những người bạn có đuôi & Thú cưng)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-011`
- **Stage**: `Stage 1: Me & My World`
- **Thematic Domain**: Pets, Animals & Emotional Bonds
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001, 002, 006, 010
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Describing Pets & Animals)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can talk about pets using 'I have a...' or 'I don't have a pet' and describe them with 'cute' and 'love'."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin nói về thú cưng của mình (chó, mèo, chim) hoặc nói mình chưa nuôi thú cưng bằng tiếng Anh!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `dog` /dɔːɡ/, `cat` /kæt/, `bird` /bɜːrd/, `fish` /fɪʃ/, `pet` /pet/, `cute` /kjuːt/, `love` /lʌv/, `animal` /ˈænɪməl/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 6 items)**: `dog`, `cat`, `bird`, `pet`, `cute`, `love`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `I have a [Pet].` (/aɪ hæv ə.../)
- `I don't have a pet.` (/aɪ doʊnt hæv ə pet/)
- `He / She is very cute.` (/hiː ɪz ˈveri kjuːt/)
- `I love my [pet].` (/aɪ lʌv maɪ.../)
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Indefinite Article with Countable Pets: `[I have] + [a] + [Singular Pet]` vs `[I have] + [two] + [Pets + s]`
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Khi nuôi 1 con vật, nhớ thêm từ đệm 'a': 'a dog', 'a cat'. Khi nuôi từ 2 con trở lên thì bỏ 'a' và thêm đuôi số nhiều: 'two cats', 'two dogs'!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Distinction between final /t/ in *cat* and final /g/ in *dog*; plural ending /s/ in *cats* vs /z/ in *dogs*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - *Cat*: "Gõ nhẹ đầu lưỡi ở cuối: ca...t /kæt/".
  - *Dog*: "Ngân nhẹ âm /g/ trong cổ họng: do...g /dɔːɡ/".

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: Cute pet plushies (1 dog, 1 cat) or funny pet photo cards, "Pet Adoption Certificates".
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Pet Shelter L-011.

---

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & Animal Sounds (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "The Secret Animal Sound" (Tiếng kêu của ai?).
- Teacher: "Woof woof! What animal is that?" $\rightarrow$ Students: "Dog!"
- Teacher: "Meow meow! What animal is that?" $\rightarrow$ Students: "Cat!"
- (Cả lớp cười vui vẻ, khởi động giọng nói)

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Room Check (Lesson 010 callback).
- Teacher: "What is in your room?" $\rightarrow$ Student: "There is a bed! There are two chairs!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Pet Shelter Story (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher tells a visual story of visiting an animal shelter.
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm chú cún nhồi bông ôm vào lòng) "Look! This is my dog. His name is Lucky. He is very cute! I love my dog!"
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào bạn mèo bông) "And this is my friend's cat. She is cute too."
  - *Teacher*: (Xua tay nhẹ nhàng) "If you don't have a dog: 'I don't have a pet.' That's okay too!"
- **Comprehension Check**: "Thầy có nuôi chó không? Chú chó tên là gì?" $\rightarrow$ "Thầy có chú chó tên Lucky rất dễ thương ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Discrimination & Final Sound Drill (Minutes 18–25)
- **Minimal Pair Check**:
  - Token A: *ca* (nuốt âm cuối) vs Token B: *cat* (gõ đầu lưỡi /t/).
  - "Con nghe thấy âm nào có tiếng gõ đầu lưỡi sắc nét? B là chú mèo chuẩn tiếng Anh!"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - Luyện âm /kjuːt/ cho từ *cute*: "Bật hơi chữ c và kết thúc bằng tiếng gõ /t/: cute!"
  - Luyện âm /lʌv/ cho từ *love*: "Răng trên chạm môi dưới ngân nhẹ: love... I love my dog!"

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Pet Shelter Adoption on LingoPro:
  - Blue Slot: `[I have]` / `[I don't have]`
  - Green Article/Number Slot: `[a]` / `[two]` / `[a pet]`
  - Yellow Pet Slot: `[dog]` `[cat]` `[bird]` `[cats]` `[dogs]`
- Students assemble their actual pet status:
  - Has 1 cat $\rightarrow$ `[I have]` + `[a]` + `[cat]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"I have a cat."*
  - Has no pets $\rightarrow$ `[I don't have]` + `[a pet]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"I don't have a pet."*
  - **Fictional freedom allowed**: If student wants a pet tiger or dragon, teacher celebrates it!

### Phase 6: Pet Adoption Fair Roleplay (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Pet Interview.
- Student A asks: "Do you have a pet?"
- Student B shows their pet drawing:
  - *Option 1*: "Yes! I have a dog. He is very cute. I love my dog!"
  - *Option 2*: "No, I don't have a pet. But I like cats!"
- Switch roles. Every pair practices 2 authentic exchanges.

### Phase 7: Exit Can-Do Ticket & Pet Hero Badge (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Each student steps up and announces their pet status to the class:
- **Exit Statement**:
  - *"Today I can say: 'I have a dog. He is cute.' OR 'I don't have a pet, but I like cats.'!"*
- Teacher awards the "Animal Hero" badge.

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| *"I have a dogs"* | Redundant article with plural. | "Nếu có 1 chú chó thì dùng 'a dog', còn nếu có nhiều chú chó thì bỏ chữ 'a' đi nhé: 'I have two dogs'!" |
| Swallowing /t/ in *cat*: *"I have a ca"* | L1 unreleased stop habit. | "Gõ nhẹ đầu lưỡi ở cuối từ cat nào: ca...t! Tuyệt vời!" |
| Sadness if student doesn't have a pet | Social sensitivity. | Immediately validate: "Nói 'I don't have a pet' là cực kỳ chuẩn tiếng Anh! Con có thể chọn nuôi một chú rồng ảo trong trò chơi hôm nay!" |

---

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Provide 2 visual choice cards (`[Have Cat]`, `[No Pet]`). Student taps and echoes the complete sentence.
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Name the pet and describe color: *"I have a black cat. His name is Luna. He is very cute!"*

---

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (LingoPro App)
- **Minute 1–2**: Open LingoPro $\rightarrow$ Lesson 011 $\rightarrow$ Listen to 3 pet descriptions.
- **Minute 3**: S-V-O Builder $\rightarrow$ Assemble 1 pet sentence.
- **Minute 4**: Tap microphone $\rightarrow$ Record: *"I love cats. They are cute."*
- **Minute 5**: Tell parents about your favorite animal in English.

---

## 8. Mastery Passing Gate
- **Possession Accuracy**: Correct distinction between `I have a [pet]` and `I don't have a pet`.
- **Pronunciation Intelligibility**: Audible /t/ on *cat* and /g/ on *dog* with $\ge 70\%$ SafeHarbor match.
