# Lesson 006: Number Magic (Số đếm kỳ diệu 1–20 & Đồ vật)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-006`
- **Stage**: `Stage 0: English Is Safe` (Stage 0 Capstone & Gateway)
- **Thematic Domain**: Numbers, Quantities & Possession
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001, 002, 005
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Counting & Describing Quantities)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can count from 1 to 10 and say how many items I have using 'I have [number] [items]' with correct plural sounds."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin đếm số lượng từ 1 đến 10 và nói mình có bao nhiêu món đồ bằng mẫu câu 'I have [số] [đồ vật]' kèm âm gió chuẩn xác!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `one` /wʌn/, `two` /tuː/, `three` /θriː/, `four` /fɔːr/, `five` /faɪv/, `six` /sɪks/, `seven` /ˈsevən/, `eight` /eɪt/, `nine` /naɪn/, `ten` /ten/, `have` /hæv/, `how many` /haʊ ˈmeni/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 7 items)**: `one`, `two`, `three`, `four`, `five`, `ten`, `have`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `I have [Number] [Plural Nouns].` (/aɪ hæv.../)
- `How many [Items] do you have?` (/haʊ ˈmeni.../)
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Quantity Frame: `[Subject] + [have] + [Number] + [Noun + s]`
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Từ 2 món trở lên (two, three...), tiếng Anh bắt buộc phải gắn thêm 'chiếc đuôi rắn xì hơi' /-s/ hoặc /-z/ vào sau danh từ: two pens, three books!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Voiceless /θ/ in *three*, final /v/ in *five / have*, and the plural snake sound /s/ on *books* and /z/ on *pens*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - *Three*: "Cắn nhẹ đầu lưỡi rồi thổi nhẹ: three /θriː/, không nói là 'tơ-ri' hay 'xi'!"
  - *Plural Ending*: "Hai bàn tay xòe ra, miệng xì hơi: two book... s!"

---

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: A box with plastic colored counting tokens (pens, marbles, or small paper clips), Number flashcards 1 to 10.
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Number Counter L-006.

---

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & High-Energy Finger Count (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "The Finger Flash Game" (Búng ngón tay đếm số).
- **Teacher Script**:
  - *Teacher*: "Chào các siêu nhân! Giơ hai bàn tay lên nào! Thầy xòe mấy ngón tay, cả lớp cùng hô to bằng tiếng Anh nhé!"
  - Teacher flashes 1 finger $\rightarrow$ "One!" ... 2 fingers $\rightarrow$ "Two!" ... 5 fingers $\rightarrow$ "Five!"
  - (Cả lớp hào hứng khởi động nhanh)

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Backpack Items Flash (Lesson 005 callback).
- Teacher holds up a ruler: "This is my...?" $\rightarrow$ Class: "ruler!"
- Teacher holds up a bag: "This is my...?" $\rightarrow$ Class: "bag!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Counting Mystery Box (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher pulls multiple pens and books out of a magic box.
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm 1 cây bút) "Look: one pen."
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm thêm cây bút thứ 2, làm động tác xì hơi nhấn mạnh vào âm đuôi) "Two pen... s! I have two pens."
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm 3 quyển sách) "One, two, three! I have three book... s! Listen to the snake sound: books!"
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm 5 cây thước) "I have five rulers."
- **Check Comprehension**: "Khi có từ 2 đồ vật trở lên, cái đuôi của từ tiếng Anh có gì đặc biệt?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "Có tiếng xì hơi /s/ hoặc /z/ ở cuối ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Ear-Check & Plural Snake Hunt (Minutes 18–25)
- **Ear-Check Game**: "Nghe bắt con rắn" (Catch the Snake).
  - Teacher plays Token 1: `book` (không có âm đuôi).
  - Teacher plays Token 2: `books` (có âm xì /s/).
  - "Con nghe thấy âm nào có con rắn? Số 2 đúng không? Nhớ bắt con rắn khi nói số nhiều nhé!"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - Luyện âm /θ/ trong số 3: "Cắn nhẹ đầu lưỡi: three... three books!"
  - Luyện âm /v/ trong số 5 và chữ have: "Răng trên chạm môi dưới ngân nhẹ: five... have... I have five pens!"

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Numerical Slot Assembly on LingoPro board:
  - Blue Slot: `[I]`
  - Orange Verb Slot: `[have]`
  - Green Number Slot: `[one]` `[two]` `[three]` `[four]` `[five]`
  - Yellow Plural Noun Slot: `[pens]` `[books]` `[rulers]` `[bags]`
- Students drag tokens to match displayed picture puzzles:
  - Picture shows 3 pens $\rightarrow$ `[I] [have] [three] [pens]` $\rightarrow$ Audio plays: *"I have three pens."*

### Phase 6: Pencil Case Inventory Challenge (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Inventory Inspection.
- Student A counts their own pens: "I have four pens! And you?"
- Student B opens their pencil case: "I have two pens and three rulers!"
- Teacher walks around, listens closely for the plural ending sounds, and awards gold stars.

### Phase 7: Stage 0 Capstone Exit Ticket & Graduation (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Stage 0 Graduation Declaration!
- Every student stands tall, smiles, and delivers their complete Stage 0 Showcase:
  - *"Hello! My name is [Name]. I'm 12 years old. I like football. I can run. I have two pens!"*
- Teacher awards the **"Stage 0: English Is Safe Graduate Certificate"**!

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Dropping plural *-s*: *"I have two book"* | Vietnamese has no plural inflection (*hai quyển sách*). | "Có 2 quyển sách rồi, nhớ thả con rắn xì hơi ở cuối từ book nhé: 'two books'!" |
| Pronouncing *three* as *tơ-ri* or *xi-ri* | L1 lack of dental fricative /θ/. | "Cắn nhẹ đầu lưỡi nào, thổi nhẹ hơi gió: three! Rất tây rồi đấy!" |
| Dropping /v/ in *have*: Pronouncing *I he* | L1 unreleased stop habit. | "Răng trên chạm nhẹ môi dưới ngân âm /v/ nào: I have... Rất tốt!" |

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Use physical plastic counting chips directly on the table. Student counts: "One, two, three pens" and echoes: "I have three pens."
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Count up to 10: *"I have ten books in my room!"*

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (LingoPro App)
- **Minute 1–2**: Open LingoPro $\rightarrow$ Lesson 006 $\rightarrow$ Listen to counting numbers 1–10.
- **Minute 3**: S-V-O Builder $\rightarrow$ Assemble 2 inventory sentences.
- **Minute 4**: Tap microphone $\rightarrow$ Count pens on your desk and record: *"I have three pens."*
- **Minute 5**: Show Stage 0 Graduate Certificate to parents.
- **Parent Tip**: *"Bố mẹ hãy tự hào chúc mừng con đã hoàn thành chặng đầu tiên. Con đã bắt đầu nói được các câu tiếng Anh hoàn chỉnh!"*

## 8. Mastery Passing Gate (Stage 0 Exit Standard)
- **Plural Accuracy**: Student audibly articulates plural ending /-s/ or /-z/ on at least $80\%$ of multi-item prompts.
- **SafeHarbor Intelligibility**: Full Stage 0 showcase achieves $\ge 70\%$ match score with clear, calm demeanor.
