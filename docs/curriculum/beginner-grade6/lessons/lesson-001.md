# Lesson 001: Who Am I? (Tôi là ai?)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-001`
- **Stage**: `Stage 0: English Is Safe`
- **Thematic Domain**: Identity & Self
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam, Pre-A1 / Below A1)
- **Prerequisites**: None (Absolute Ground Zero)
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Personal Identity)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**: 
  - *EN*: "I can say my name, my age, and ask a friend's name in a simple 2-way dialogue."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin nói tên, tuổi và hỏi tên người đối diện bằng tiếng Anh!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `hello` /həˈloʊ/, `bye` /baɪ/, `name` /neɪm/, `twelve` /twelv/, `eleven` /ɪˈlevən/, `year` /jɪər/, `old` /oʊld/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 4 items)**: `name`, `twelve` (hoặc `eleven`), `hello`, `bye`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `Hello!` (/həˈloʊ/)
- `My name is [Name].` (/maɪ neɪm ɪz.../)
- `I'm [11 / 12].` (/aɪm twelv/) — *(Accept "I'm 12" directly; "years old" is optional)*
- `What's your name?` (/wɑːts jʊər neɪm/) — *(Interactive communicative question)*
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Invariant Subject Pronoun + Copula Block: `I'm...` (treated as a single melodic block /aɪm/, zero conjugation tables).
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Để nói về chính mình, con chỉ cần dùng khối quen thuộc 'I'm' (Tôi là...). Muốn hỏi tên bạn, chỉ cần một câu hỏi siêu tiện: 'What's your name?'"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Ending lip closure /m/ in *name*.
- **Articulatory Support (OPTIONAL CUE — PILOT AND OBSERVE)**:
  - *Ending /m/ in name*: Gently close lips at the end of the word (/neɪm/). If the learner finds this cue awkward or silly, drop it immediately and use natural auditory modeling.

---

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: Name badge stickers, 1 small soft ball (or hand puppet).
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Slide Deck L-001, 0.8x audio player.
- **Teacher Emotional Posture**: Broad smile, open body language, gentle volume, zero intimidating posture.

---

## 4. Realistic Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional De-escalation & Welcome Warm-Up (Minutes 0–8)
- **Objective**: Establish psychological safety and demonstrate that this class does not judge or grade.
- **Activity**: "The Wave & Smile Game" (Trò chơi vẫy tay mỉm cười).
- **Teacher Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Vẫy tay nhẹ, cười tươi) "Hello everyone! Vẫy tay chào thầy nào!"
  - *Students*: (Vẫy tay lại)
  - *Teacher*: "Các con thấy không, từ đầu tiên của buổi học là 'Hello' - từ này ai cũng biết rồi đúng không? Hôm nay chúng mình không có bài kiểm tra, không chấm điểm, chỉ cùng nhau khám phá xem tiếng Anh dễ như thế nào nhé!"

### Phase 2: Comprehensible Input & Avatar Discovery (Minutes 8–18)
- **Objective**: Acoustic listening exposure before text decoding.
- **Activity**: Teacher introduces avatar on screen: "Super Nam".
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Avatar Audio (0.8x)*: "Hello! My name is Nam. I'm 12. What's your name?"
  - Teacher plays audio 2 times. Points to name badge, shows 12 fingers, points forward to student with a friendly smile.
- **Teacher Prompt**: "Bạn trên màn hình vừa nói bạn ấy tên là gì nhỉ? Có bạn nào nghe được không?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "Nam ạ!"
- **Teacher**: "Chính xác! Nam! Bạn ấy bao nhiêu tuổi? 10 hay 12?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "12 ạ!"

### Phase 3: Mouth Gym & Acoustic Practice (Minutes 18–26)
- **Objective**: Acoustic perception and physical awareness for final /m/ in *name*.
- **Minimal Pair Check**:
  - Token A: *nay* (không ngậm môi) vs Token B: *name* (ngậm môi có âm /m/).
  - Teacher demonstrates: "Con nhìn miệng thầy này: na... me (ngậm môi nhẹ). Cùng thử ngậm môi phát âm nhé: /neɪm/!"
- **Choral Practice**: Whole class repeats softly: `My name... My name is...`

### Phase 4: S-V-O Sentence Cards & Low-Stakes Practice (Minutes 26–38)
- **Objective**: Visual sentence assembly and interactive 2-way practice.
- **Activity 1**: Magnetic or digital word cards:
  - Cards: `[My name is]` (Blue) + `[Minh]` (Green).
  - Cards: `[I'm]` (Blue) + `[12]` (Orange).
  - Cards: `[What's your name?]` (Purple Interactive Question).
- **Activity 2**: Rolling soft ball or puppet game:
  - *Teacher*: "Hello! My name is Alex. (Lăn bóng nhẹ) What's your name?"
  - *Student*: "My name is... Minh. (Hỏi lại thầy) What's your name?"
  - *Teacher*: (Bắt tay vui vẻ) "My name is Alex! Tuyệt vời!"
  - *Praise & Support*: If student hesitates, offer immediate binary choice (*"Minh hay Nam?"*).

### Phase 5: Exit Can-Do Ticket & Victory High-Five (Minutes 38–45)
- **Objective**: Authentic 2-turn communicative exchange.
- **Activity**: Student conducts a complete mini-dialogue with teacher:
  - Student: *"Hello! My name is [Name]. I'm 12. What's your name?"*
  - Teacher: *"Hello [Name]! My name is Alex!"*
- Teacher awards the "Identity Badge" sticker and gives a high-five.

---

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Linguistic Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Student drops *is*: *"My name Minh"* | Vietnamese syntax: *Tên tôi Minh* (no copula). | "Gần đúng rồi con! Con thêm chiếc cầu nối 'is' vào giữa nhé: 'My name is Minh'. Thử lại nào!" |
| Student swallows /m/: *"My nay is"* | Vietnamese unreleased coda habit. | "Nhớ ngậm môi ở cuối chữ name nhé: na-me... Rất tốt!" |
| Student omits *years old*: *"I'm 12"* | Natural developmental economy. | **Accept "I'm 12" as 100% valid!** Praise immediately. Do not force "years old" at this stage. |

---

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway (Struggling Learner)**:
  - Allow student to simply hold up their name badge while teacher says: "Your name is Minh!" Student nods and echoes: "My name is Minh."
- **High-Flyer Pathway (Quick Learner)**:
  - Challenge student to add their school: *"I'm a student at Nguyen Du School."*
