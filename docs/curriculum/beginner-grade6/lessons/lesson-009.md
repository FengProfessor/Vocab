# Lesson 009: Meet My Friend (Gặp gỡ bạn thân của con)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-009`
- **Stage**: `Stage 1: Me & My World`
- **Thematic Domain**: Friendship, People & Personal Qualities
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001, 005, 007, 008
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Describing Other People)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can introduce my friend, stating their name, age, and one personality trait using 'He/She is...'."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin giới thiệu bạn thân của mình (tên, tuổi, tính cách tốt bụng/vui vẻ) bằng mẫu câu 'This is my friend... He/She is...'!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `friend` /frend/, `best friend` /best frend/, `he` /hiː/, `she` /ʃiː/, `funny` /ˈfʌni/, `kind` /kaɪnd/, `smart` /smɑːrt/, `nice` /naɪs/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 6 items)**: `friend`, `he`, `she`, `funny`, `kind`, `smart`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `This is my friend, [Name].` (/ðɪs ɪz maɪ frend.../)
- `He is [11 / 12].` (/hiː ɪz.../)
- `She is [11 / 12].` (/ʃiː ɪz.../)
- `He / She is very [funny / kind / smart].` (/hiː ɪz ˈveri.../)
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: 3rd Person Singular Subject Frame: `[He / She] + [is] + [Adjective]`
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Khi giới thiệu một bạn nam, ta dùng 'He' (anh ấy/cậu ấy). Khi giới thiệu một bạn nữ, ta dùng 'She' (cô ấy/bạn ấy). Cả hai đều đi chung với cây cầu 'is'!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Consonant cluster /fr/ in *friend* and /ʃ/ in *she*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - */fr/ in friend*: "Răng trên chạm nhẹ môi dưới /f/ rồi lướt mượt mà sang âm /r/: fr... friend, không đọc là 'phơ-ren'!".
  - *She*: "Tròn môi làm dấu suỵt: sh... she /ʃiː/!".

---

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: Avatar cards of funny cartoon characters (boys and girls), Blank mini picture frame cards for students to draw their friend.
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Best Friend Showcase L-009.

---

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & Buddy High-Five (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "The Double High-Five" (Đập tay cùng bạn).
- Teacher: "Chào cả lớp! Quay sang người bạn ngồi cạnh, nở một nụ cười thật tươi và đập tay hai phát nào!"
- (Tiếng cười và không khí thân thiện lan tỏa khắp lớp)
- Teacher: "Hôm nay chúng mình sẽ cùng học cách khoe người bạn tuyệt vời nhất của mình bằng tiếng Anh nhé!"

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Outfit Spotting (Lesson 008 callback).
- Teacher points to a student: "What color is your shirt?"
- Student: "My shirt is white! I wear black shoes!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Best Friend Story (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher introduces two best friends on screen: "Nam" and "Mai".
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào bạn nam đeo kính cười tươi) "Look! This is my friend, Nam. He is 12 years old. He is very funny! He tells great jokes!"
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ vào bạn nữ ôm sách mỉm cười) "And this is my friend, Mai. She is 12 years old. She is very kind and smart!"
  - Teacher repeats twice, pointing clearly to the boy for *He* and the girl for *She*.
- **Comprehension Check**: "Bạn nam tên gì? Dùng He hay She? Bạn nữ tên gì? Dùng He hay She?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "Bạn nam là Nam dùng He; bạn nữ là Mai dùng She ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Discrimination & Gender Switch Drill (Minutes 18–25)
- **Acoustic Check**:
  - Teacher plays: Token A: *He* /hiː/ vs Token B: *She* /ʃiː/.
  - "Con nghe thấy âm nào có tiếng suỵt? B là She (bạn nữ)!"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - "Luyện từ friend nào: Răng chạm môi dưới lướt êm ái: fr... friend!"
  - "Luyện 3 tính từ khen bạn: funny (hài hước), kind (tốt bụng), smart (thông minh)!"

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Best Friend Builder on LingoPro board:
  - Subject Slot: `[He]` (Blue) / `[She]` (Pink)
  - Verb Slot: `[is]`
  - Adjective Slot: `[funny]` `[kind]` `[smart]` `[tall]`
- Students drag chips to match displayed character cards:
  - Example: Picture of a smiling girl $\rightarrow$ `[She]` + `[is]` + `[kind]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"She is kind."*
  - Example: Picture of a funny boy $\rightarrow$ `[He]` + `[is]` + `[funny]` $\rightarrow$ Audio: *"He is funny."*

### Phase 6: Best Friend Showcase & Mini-Profile (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Friend Introduction.
- Every student takes 1 minute to draw a funny smiley face of their real best friend on a card.
- Student A shows their card to Student B:
  - *Student A*: "This is my friend, Minh. He is 12 years old. He is very funny!"
  - *Student B*: "Cool! This is my friend, Lan. She is 11 years old. She is very smart!"
- Switch roles. Teacher walks around giving golden "Best Buddy" stamps.

### Phase 7: Exit Can-Do Ticket & Best Friend Badge (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Each student steps up and introduces their friend to the teacher:
- **Exit Statement**:
  - *"Today I can say: 'This is my friend, [Name]. He/She is 12. He/She is very kind.'!"*
- Teacher awards the "Best Friend Ambassador" badge.

---

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Confusing *He* and *She* (Calling a boy *she*) | L1 interference (Vietnamese pronoun *bạn ấy* is gender-neutral). | "Bạn nam đẹp trai dùng 'He' con nhé! Nhớ câu thần chú: He là anh ấy, She là cô ấy!" |
| Omitting *is*: *"He funny"* | Vietnamese syntax: *Cậu ấy vui tính* (no copula). | "Thêm chiếc cầu nhỏ 'is' nối lại nào con: 'He is funny'! Rất chuẩn xác!" |
| Pronouncing *friend* as *phơ-ren* | Breaking consonant cluster /fr/. | "Trượt môi êm ái nào con: fr... friend! Nghe tây hơn rất nhiều!" |

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Provide 2 pre-drawn character cards (1 boy, 1 girl). Student points and echoes: "This is my friend. He is funny."
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Connect with a like: *"He is my friend. He likes football and games."*

## 7. 5-Minute Micro-Homework (LingoPro App)
- **Minute 1–2**: Open LingoPro $\rightarrow$ Lesson 009 $\rightarrow$ Listen to 3 friend descriptions.
- **Minute 3**: Match pronouns `He` and `She` to 4 character avatars.
- **Minute 4**: Tap microphone $\rightarrow$ Record: *"This is my friend, Nam. He is funny."*
- **Minute 5**: Tell mom about your best friend in English.

## 8. Mastery Passing Gate
- **Pronoun Agreement**: $100\%$ correct usage of `He` for boys and `She` for girls.
- **Speech Delivery**: Clear articulation of `This is my friend... He/She is...` with $\ge 70\%$ SafeHarbor score.
