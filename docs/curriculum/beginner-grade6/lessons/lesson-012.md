# Lesson 012: What Is That? (Cái đó là cái gì vậy? & Phân biệt Gần/Xa)

## 1. Lesson Metadata
- **Lesson ID**: `L-012`
- **Stage**: `Stage 1: Me & My World` (Stage 1 Capstone & Gateway)
- **Thematic Domain**: Inquiring, Distance Deixis & Classroom Objects
- **Target Age**: 11–12 years old (Grade 6 Vietnam)
- **Prerequisites**: Lessons 001–011 (Complete Stage 0 & 1)
- **CEFR Reference**: Pre-A1 (Asking Simple Information Questions)
- **Student Can-Do Outcome**:
  - *EN*: "I can ask 'What's this?' for objects near me and 'What's that?' for objects far away, and answer 'It's a...'."
  - *VI*: "Hôm nay con có thể tự tin chủ động chỉ tay hỏi 'What's this?' (gần) và 'What's that?' (xa) cũng như trả lời đồ vật bằng tiếng Anh!"

---

## 2. Target Linguistic Arsenal
### A. Vocabulary Inventory
- **Receptive Exposure**: `what` /wɒt/, `that` /ðæt/, `clock` /klɑːk/, `board` /bɔːrd/, `phone` /foʊn/, `box` /bɑːks/, `near` /nɪər/, `far` /fɑːr/.
- **Productive Target (Strict 6 items)**: `what`, `that`, `clock`, `board`, `phone`, `box`.
### B. Invariant Formulaic Chunks
- `What's this?` (/wɒts ðɪs/) - "Cái này là cái gì?" (ở gần)
- `What's that?` (/wɒts ðæt/) - "Cái kia là cái gì?" (ở xa)
- `It's a [Item].` (/ɪts ə.../) - "Đó là một..."
### C. Grammar Support (Inductive)
- **Pattern**: Spatial Deixis Distinction:
  - Near (chạm tay được): `What's this?` $\rightarrow$ `It's a [noun].`
  - Far (chỉ tay từ xa): `What's that?` $\rightarrow$ `It's a [noun].`
- **Micro-Rule (VI)**: *"Ở ngay trong tay hoặc sát bên cạnh, con hỏi 'What's this?'. Ở đằng xa con phải chỉ ngón tay, con hỏi 'What's that?'. Trả lời đều bắt đầu bằng 'It's a...'!"*
### D. Pronunciation Focus
- **Target Sound**: Falling Wh-question intonation (`What's this? ↘`, `What's that? ↘`) and final cluster /ks/ in *box*.
- **Physical Mnemonic**:
  - *Wh-Intonation*: "Đầu câu cao giọng, cuối câu hạ giọng dứt khoát như một mũi tên cắm xuống: What's this? ↘".
  - *Box*: "Bật âm /k/ rồi xì âm /s/ liền mạch như tiếng búng tay: bo-k-s /bɑːks/!".

---

## 3. Teacher Preparation & Resources
- **Classroom Props**: A laser pointer or long wooden pointer stick, 1 wall clock, 1 phone, 1 small mystery box.
- **Digital Assets**: LingoPro Interactive Distance Detective Slide L-012.

---

## 4. Minute-by-Minute Lesson Plan (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Emotional Warm-Up & The Pointing Stretch (Minutes 0–5)
- **Activity**: "Near Touch vs Far Point" (Chạm gần - Chỉ xa).
- Teacher taps the table right in front: "Near! Chạm vào bàn!" $\rightarrow$ Students touch their desks.
- Teacher points to the door across the room: "Far! Chỉ ngón tay ra cửa!" $\rightarrow$ Students point across the room.
- (Khởi động thể chất, làm rõ khái niệm không gian)

### Phase 2: Spaced Retrieval Warm-Up (Minutes 5–10)
- **Activity**: Pet & Room Flash (Lessons 010–011 callback).
- Teacher: "Who has a dog?" $\rightarrow$ Student: "I have a dog. He is cute!"
- Teacher: "What is in your room?" $\rightarrow$ Student: "There is a bed!"

### Phase 3: Comprehensible Input & The Distance Detective (Minutes 10–18)
- **Activity**: Teacher demonstrates near vs far with dramatic gestures.
- **Listening Script**:
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm chiếc điện thoại ngay trên tay, nhìn tò mò) "What's this? ... Oh! It's a phone."
  - *Teacher*: (Cầm chiếc hộp giấy nhỏ ngay trên bàn) "What's this? ... It's a box."
  - *Teacher*: (Đi lùi lại 3 mét, chỉ tay lên chiếc đồng hồ treo tường cao ở góc lớp) "Look over there! What's that? ... It's a clock."
  - *Teacher*: (Chỉ tay lên chiếc bảng đen lớn ở đầu lớp) "What's that? ... It's a board."
- **Check Comprehension**: "Khi nào thầy hỏi 'What's this?' và khi nào thầy hỏi 'What's that?'?"
- **Expected Student Response**: "Cầm trên tay thì hỏi What's this; ở đằng xa chỉ tay thì hỏi What's that ạ!"

### Phase 4: Acoustic Discrimination & Intonation Melody (Minutes 18–25)
- **Intonation Ear-Check**:
  - Teacher demonstrates:
    - Robot voice (Flat): *What is this.* (Nghe chán và không tự nhiên).
    - Natural melody (Down-arrow): *What's this? ↘* (Nghe sống động và tò mò).
  - "Cả lớp cùng hạ giọng cuối câu như mũi tên nào: What's this? ↘ ... What's that? ↘"
- **Mouth Gym**:
  - Luyện âm /ks/ trong từ *box*: "Bật k rồi xì s: box /bɑːks/!"
  - Luyện âm /ðæt/ trong từ *that*: "Rung đầu lưỡi và gõ nhẹ âm /t/: that!"

### Phase 5: S-V-O Sentence Builder (Minutes 25–33)
- **Activity**: Distance Detective Puzzle on LingoPro:
  - Green Question Slot: `[What's this?]` (Biểu tượng bàn tay chạm) / `[What's that?]` (Biểu tượng ngón tay chỉ xa)
  - Blue Answer Slot: `[It's a]`
  - Yellow Object Slot: `[phone]` `[box]` `[clock]` `[board]` `[bag]` `[pen]`
- Students match questions and answers to distance scenarios:
  - Hand holding a phone $\rightarrow$ `What's this?` $\rightarrow$ `It's a phone.`
  - Finger pointing to distant clock $\rightarrow$ `What's that?` $\rightarrow$ `It's a clock.`

### Phase 6: Classroom Detective Game (Minutes 33–40)
- **Activity**: Pair Mystery Quest.
- Student A picks up an item on their desk:
  - *Student A*: "What's this?"
  - *Student B*: "It's a pen!"
- Student A then points across the room to the wall clock:
  - *Student A*: "And what's that?"
  - *Student B*: "It's a clock!"
- Switch roles. Every pair practices 4 distance inquiries.

### Phase 7: Stage 1 Capstone Exit Ticket & Detective Badge (Minutes 40–45)
- **Activity**: Complete Stage 1 Capstone Showcase!
- Every student executes 1 near inquiry and 1 far inquiry with teacher:
- **Exit Statement**:
  - *"Today I can say: 'What's this? It's a phone. What's that? It's a clock.'!"*
- Teacher awards the **"Stage 1: Me & My World Graduate Badge"**!

## 5. Typical Learner Errors & Empathetic Corrections
| Error Observed | Root Cause | Empathetic Teacher Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Asking *"What's this?"* while pointing across the room | Over-generalizing *this* to all distances. | Point your finger far away: "Đồ vật ở đằng xa tít kia mà con, đổi sang chiếc kính viễn vọng 'that' nào: 'What's that?'!" |
| Rising intonation on Wh-question: *What's this? ↗* | Confusing with Yes/No question intonation. | "Câu hỏi có từ What thì hạ giọng xuống ở cuối con nhé: What's this? ↘. Giống như một tiếng thở phào nhẹ nhõm!" |
| Pronouncing *box* as *bót* | Dropping final cluster /ks/. | "Bật k rồi xì s nào con: bo-k-s... box! Rất tuyệt!" |

## 6. Differentiation Pathways
- **Soft-Landing Pathway**: Provide 2 physical distance cards (`[Touch Near]`, `[Point Far]`). Student taps the card and echoes the question model.
- **High-Flyer Pathway**: Combine with a color descriptor: *"What's that? - It's a big black clock."*

- **Minute 3**: Match 3 distance photos to `What's this?` vs `What's that?`.

## 8. Mastery Passing Gate (Stage 1 Exit Standard)
- **Deictic Precision**: $100\%$ accurate distinction between near (*this*) and far (*that*) prompts.
- **Oral Intonation**: Correct falling contour ($\searrow$) on Wh-questions with $\ge 70\%$ SafeHarbor match.
