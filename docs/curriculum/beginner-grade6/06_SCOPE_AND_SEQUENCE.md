# 06. Scope & Sequence: 42-Unit Mastery Architecture

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Authors**: Curriculum Architect (Role A), Learning Science Specialist (Role D), Synthesis Lead (Role L)  
> **Core Principle**: **PROGRESS BY MASTERY, NOT BY PASSING CALENDAR TIME.** Students advance across stages only when empirical exit standards are satisfied.

---

## 1. Curriculum Architecture Overview

The curriculum is structured into **7 Stages**, comprising **42 Modular Units/Lessons**. Every lesson is calibrated to take an anxious 11–12-year-old Vietnamese learner from affective freezing to autonomous basic communication and seamless school reintegration.

```
STAGE 0: English Is Safe (Lessons 001–006) ────────► Affective Reset & Survival Language
STAGE 1: Me & My World (Lessons 007–012) ──────────► Self, Preferences & Immediate Environment
STAGE 2: People & Things Around Me (013–018) ──────► Family, Spatial Relations & Physical Traits
STAGE 3: My Everyday Life (Lessons 019–024) ───────► Habits, Schedules, Weather & Hobbies
STAGE 4: I Can Ask & Answer (Lessons 025–030) ─────► Wh-Questions, Social Agency & Suggestions
STAGE 5: Connected Language (Lessons 031–036) ─────► Connectors (and/but/because), Stories
STAGE 6: Bridge to School English (037–042) ───────► Global Success Grade 6 (Units 1–3 Integration)
```

## 3. Pacing & Schedule Projections

We reject the false promise of "Master English in 2 weeks". Language acquisition requires synaptic consolidation and spaced review. We provide three empirically grounded pacing models:

### Track A: Regular School Supplementary Pace (2 Sessions / Week)
- **Structure**: 2 guided sessions (45–60 min) per week + 5 min daily LingoPro FSRS review.
- **Velocity**: 2 units per week.
- **Total Duration**: **21 Calendar Weeks (~5 Months)**.
- **Recommended For**: Typical Grade 6 student balancing school workload and homework.

### Track B: Accelerated Intensive Remedial Pace (3 Sessions / Week)
- **Structure**: 3 guided sessions (45 min) per week + 8 min daily LingoPro practice.
- **Velocity**: 3 units per week.
- **Total Duration**: **14 Calendar Weeks (~3.5 Months)**.
- **Recommended For**: Students during summer vacation or intensive after-school recovery centers.

### Track C: Blended Micro-Learning Pace (5 Micro-Sessions / Week)
- **Structure**: 5 app-based micro-sessions (20 min) per week + 1 weekly live teacher checkpoint (45 min).
- **Velocity**: ~4 units per 3 weeks.
- **Total Duration**: **10–12 Calendar Weeks (~2.5–3 Months)**.
- **Recommended For**: Independent self-paced digital learners supported by parents.

### Actual Learning Hours Breakdown:
- **Total Guided Instructional Hours**: $42 \text{ units} \times 50 \text{ minutes} = \mathbf{35 \text{ Guided Hours}}$.
- **Total Spaced Retrieval App Practice**: $42 \text{ units} \times 4 \text{ FSRS review cycles} \times 7 \text{ minutes} = \mathbf{19.6 \text{ Practice Hours}}$.
- **Cumulative Investment**: $\mathbf{\approx 55 \text{ Total Active Learning Hours}}$ to bridge an absolute beginner to solid A1 autonomy.

## 4. Stage Exit Standards (Gating Criteria)

A student cannot be advanced to the next stage merely because time has elapsed. Every stage enforces explicit, objective exit gates:

- **Stage 0 Exit Gate**: Student must achieve $\ge 85\%$ accuracy on S-V-O word chip builder for `I like...` and `I have...`, and speak their name, age, and 2 likes with $\ge 70\%$ SafeHarbor intelligibility.
- **Stage 1 Exit Gate**: Student accurately identifies 10/12 feelings, clothes, and colors, and introduces their best friend in 3 coherent spoken sentences.
- **Stage 2 Exit Gate**: Student correctly follows and gives 4-step directions on a map and successfully places 5 objects using spatial prepositions (*on/in/under/next to*).
- **Stage 3 Exit Gate**: Student accurately sequences their morning routine and describes 3 continuous actions in progress (`be + V-ing`) without dropping the auxiliary verb.
- **Stage 4 Exit Gate**: Student conducts a spontaneous 3-question survey using `Do you...?` and `Where is...?` and responds with appropriate short answers.
- **Stage 5 Exit Gate**: Student reads and accurately retells a 5-sentence story and writes a 5-line personal profile with zero capital-letter or sentence-boundary errors.
- **Stage 6 Exit Gate**: Student achieves $\ge 7.5/10$ on a simulated Grade 6 Unit 1–3 school exam and demonstrates self-correction of 3rd person singular *-s*.
