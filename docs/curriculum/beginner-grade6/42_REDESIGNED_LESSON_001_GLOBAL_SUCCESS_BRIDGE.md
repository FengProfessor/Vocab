> **Document Class**: Teacher-Executable Lesson Blueprint & Diagnostic Run-sheet

> **Target Learner**: 11–12 year old Vietnamese Grade-6 student (False Beginner / Broken Foundation)

## 1. LESSON OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC MISSION

### Why Old Lesson 1 Failed the 12-Year-Old
In the previous draft, Lesson 1 consisted of introducing oneself (*"Hello, I am Nam. I am 12 years old."*), passing a soft ball, receiving cartoon stickers, and repeating single isolated nouns (*"bag"*, *"book"*). A real 12-year-old felt infantilized, bored, and saw zero relevance to the scary English textbook waiting on their secondary school desk.

### The New Mission: "Survive Your First Day at School"
We transform the session into a high-stakes, age-appropriate simulation:
* **The Story Context**: You are stepping through the school gates into your new secondary school (THCS). The school bag is heavy, the teacher speaks English, and classmates are comparing their new stationery and books (mirroring Phong, Vy, and Duy in Global Success Unit 1).
* **Your Goal**: Complete 4 critical survival missions to earn your official **Grade 6 Student Explorer Badge**.

## 3. TEACHER RUN-SHEET: STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION

### PHASE 0: MISSION BRIEFING & RAPPORT (3 minutes)
* **Goal**: Establish emotional safety, remove test anxiety, and set the mission frame.
* **Teacher Script (Warm, conversational, bilingual scaffold)**:
  > *"Chào em! Hôm nay chúng ta không làm bài kiểm tra, cũng không chấm điểm số gì cả. Hôm nay là ngày đầu tiên em bước chân vào lớp 6. Em nhìn xem, sách giáo khoa Tiếng Anh 6 mở ra bằng hình ảnh bạn Phong, bạn Vy và bạn Duy mang chiếc cặp rất nặng đến trường mới. Thử thách của em hôm nay là: **Vượt qua ngày đầu tiên tại trường THCS** qua 4 nhiệm vụ sinh tồn. Em đã sẵn sàng nhận nhiệm vụ chưa?"*
* **Learner Action**: Learner taps **[ ACCEPT MISSION ]** on screen.

---

### PHASE 1: MISSION A — THE SCHOOL BAG CHALLENGE (10 minutes)
* **Context**: You must pack your school bag for the first day of Grade 6.

#### Step 1.1: Core Anchor Probe (Level F — Accessible Floor)
* **Interaction**: System plays audio and displays canonical diagnostic visuals:
  1. Audio: *"A school bag"* $\rightarrow$ Displays `bag_v1.jpg` vs 2 distractor shapes.
  2. Audio: *"A pen"* $\rightarrow$ Displays `pen_v1.jpg` vs distractor.
  3. Audio: *"A book"* $\rightarrow$ Displays `book_v1.jpg` vs distractor.
  4. Audio: *"A water bottle / drink"* $\rightarrow$ Displays `drink_v1.jpg`.
* **Adaptive Branching**:
  - *If learner identifies all 3 items quickly and independently without hesitation*:
    $\rightarrow$ **TRIGGER FAST UPWARD ADAPTATION**: Skip further Level F items immediately! Jump to Step 1.2 (Contrast Boundary).
  - *If learner hesitates or misidentifies*:
    $\rightarrow$ Run auditory echo scaffold (*"Listen closely: /b-æ-g/ - bag. Try tapping it again."*).

#### Step 1.2: The Lexical Boundary Trap (Level B — Bridge Layer)
* **Cognitive Challenge**: In Global Success 6, students constantly mix up `pen` vs `pencil` and `book` vs `notebook`.
* **Task A: Pen vs Pencil Duel**:
  - Screen displays `pen_v1.jpg` side-by-side with a wooden pencil.
  - Audio prompts: *"Find the pen that writes with blue ink."*
  - **Epistemic Strictness Rule**:
    - If learner selects `pencil`: System acknowledges: *"Đó là cây bút chì (pencil) bằng gỗ! Cây bút mực (pen) nằm ở bên này cơ."*
    - Telemetry logs: `raw_response: "pencil"`, `active_gap: "PEN_PENCIL_CONTRAST"`. No red penalty banner, but gap is recorded for spaced review.
* **Task B: Book vs Notebook Duel**:
  - Displays a printed textbook (`book_v1.jpg`) and a blank lined notebook.
  - Audio: *"Vy opens her notebook to write notes. Which one is the notebook?"*
  - Telemetry logs exact lexical boundary distinction.

#### Step 1.3: The Stretch Optimization Challenge (Level X — High Cognitive Ceiling)
* **The 30-Second School Bag Weight Puzzle**:
  - Narrative: *"Tomorrow is Tuesday. Your timetable has: **Maths, English, Art**. Your school bag has a maximum weight limit of 3 kg. Select the necessary items without overloading your bag!"*
  - Items available with weights and labels:
    - `Maths book` (0.5 kg)
    - `English notebook` (0.4 kg)
    - `Drawing pad & pencils` (0.8 kg)
    - `Heavy comic book` (1.5 kg — Trap item!)
    - `Water bottle` (0.6 kg)
    - `Pencil case with pens` (0.3 kg)
  - **Cognitive demand**: Strategic selection, reading labels in English, basic addition under 3kg constraint.
  - **Pedagogical outcome**: Proves that a false beginner can execute high-level strategic reasoning in English.

---

### PHASE 2: MISSION B — DECODE THE TEACHER (8 minutes)
* **Context**: The school bell rings. The teacher enters the Grade 6 classroom and speaks English!

#### Step 2.1: Classroom Imperatives Audio Reaction (Level B & S)
* The student hears authentic teacher audio instructions and must react within a generous window:
  1. *"Class, please open your books to page 6."* $\rightarrow$ Learner taps the book icon and swipes open.
  2. *"Take out your pens and write your name."* $\rightarrow$ Learner selects the pen and drags it to the name card.
  3. *"Close your bags, please."* $\rightarrow$ Learner taps the zipper icon on the bag.
  4. *"Put down your pencil and look at the board."* $\rightarrow$ Learner taps eyes on whiteboard.
* **Diagnostic Telemetry Recorded**:
  - `CLASSROOM_COMMAND_RECEPTIVE_LATENCY`
  - `ACTION_VERB_DISCRIMINATION` (`open` vs `close`, `take out` vs `put down`, `write` vs `read`).
  - *If learner freezes*: Teacher offers auditory pause scaffold, repeating the operative verb with gesture.

---

### PHASE 3: MISSION C — THE LANGUAGE PUZZLE (8 minutes)
* **Context**: Phong and Duy are assembling sentences about their first day at school.

#### Step 3.1: Tactile S-V-O Sentence Builder (Level S — School Ready)
To cure Vietnamese word-for-word translation ("Syntax Blindness"), the learner uses color-coded syntax blocks:
- **Blue Slots**: Subjects (`I`, `We`, `They`, `Phong`)
- **Orange Slots**: School Verbs (`have`, `study`, `wear`, `do`)
- **Green Slots**: Objects / Complements (`new books`, `uniforms`, `English`, `homework`)

#### Target Construction Challenges:
1. **Challenge 1 (Possession)**:
   - Target: `[I] [have] [new books]` (Matches Global Success: *"I have new books"*)
   - Contrastive distractor block: `[am]` (Catches the common Vietnamese error: *"I am new books"*).
2. **Challenge 2 (School Identity)**:
   - Target: `[We] [wear] [uniforms]`
   - Distractor block: `[study]`
3. **Challenge 3 (Curriculum)**:
   - Target: `[I] [study] [English and Maths]`

* **System Behavior**:
  - Snapping blocks into place triggers synthesized sentence playback: *"I have new books."*
  - Child can tap individual words to hear them isolated or hear the whole fluent chunk.

---

### PHASE 4: MISSION D — CRAFT YOUR SCHOOL IDENTITY BADGE (6 minutes)
* **Context**: The school principal gives you an official Explorer Identity Card to customize.

#### Step 4.1: Creative Personalization (Level S & X)
* The student fills their digital school badge:
  - **Avatar**: Choose or design a character.
  - **Statement 1**: *"My name is [Student Name]."*
  - **Statement 2 (In my bag)**: Select 2 items they actually have: *"In my bag, I have [a blue pen] and [a big notebook]."* (Fictional/humorous options allowed: e.g. *a pet dragon*!).
  - **Statement 3 (Subjects)**: *"I study [English] and [Science]."*
* **SafeHarbor Speech Scoring (Zero Stress)**:
  - Student records their voice reading their badge statements.
  - System evaluates phonological features:
    - Final consonant release: /k/ in `book`, /z/ in `pens`.
    - Lexical stress: `UN-i-form`, `ENG-lish`.
  - Immediate affirmative feedback celebrating badge completion!

## 5. POST-LESSON ROUTING DECISION

Based on evidence collected:
* **IF Learner solved Level F instantly & completed Level S**:
  $\rightarrow$ Update School Readiness Status to **`BRIDGE READY / SCHOOL READY`** for Unit 1.
  $\rightarrow$ Next lesson advances immediately to Unit 1 A Closer Look 1 (Subjects & /ɑː/ vs /ʌ/ pronunciation).
* **IF Learner exhibited Lexical Boundary Gaps (`pen/pencil`)**:
  $\rightarrow$ System injects a 3-minute micro-review in Lesson 2 before proceeding.
* **IF Learner exhibited Receptive Command Gaps**:
  $\rightarrow$ System reinforces classroom imperatives through interactive mini-games in Lesson 2.
