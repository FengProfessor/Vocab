## 1. Executive Summary

This repository contains the complete, scientifically grounded curriculum architecture designed to take an 11–12-year-old Vietnamese student who is terrified of English, suffering from learned helplessness, and unable to construct a basic sentence, from **"Con ghét tiếng Anh / Con không làm được"** to **autonomous communicative agency and successful integration into the national Grade 6 English textbook (Global Success)**.

### Core Architecture Highlights:
- **THIS IS NOT A SPEAKING-ONLY COURSE**: Speaking is one modality, not the sole definition of language acquisition. The curriculum integrates meaning, vocabulary, listening, reading, tactile assembly, speaking, writing, grammar, review, and diagnosis.
- **THIS IS NOT A 3000-WORD MEMORIZATION COURSE**: Rejects random noun hoarding (e.g. 50 zoo animals). Prioritizes high **Generative Power** ($GP$)—core action verbs (`want`, `have`, `like`), question words (`what`, `where`), and functional relators that empower sentence generation.
- **THIS IS NOT A GRAMMAR-FIRST COURSE**: Replaces abstract formula dumping ($S + V(s/es) + O$) with inductive, high-utility formulaic chunks and tactile word-chip assembly.
- **DUAL ARCHITECTURE (TEACHING + DIAGNOSIS)**: Teaching and diagnosis occur simultaneously. Diagnosis is not a stressful upfront placement test, but continuous, low-pressure micro-probes (2–4 per lesson) that update an inspectable Vocabulary Gap Map.
- **MULTIDIMENSIONAL VOCABULARY EVIDENCE**: Rejects binary "Known vs Unknown". Tracks 8 independent evidence dimensions (`HEARD_RECOGNIZED`, `TEXT_RECOGNIZED`, `FORM_RECALLED`, `CHUNK_USED`, etc.) and classifies breakdowns into 10 explicit gap types.
- **VISUAL ASSETS ARE EVIDENCE INSTRUMENTS**: Visuals must have `V0` clarity with zero text leakage and age-appropriate dignity (11–12yo secondary school). Ambiguous images are flagged as instrument errors, never penalizing the student's mastery score.
