## 1. THE FIVE-TIER DIAGNOSTIC TRACEABILITY MATRIX

When a Grade-6 learner fails a task at school, traditional schools simply mark a red "X" and record a low score (e.g., 3/10). LingoPro executes an epistemically strict diagnosis that unpacks the exact structural failure point.

## 2. COMPREHENSIVE SCHOOL-BRIDGE TRACEABILITY TABLE

| # | Global Success 6 Demand | Foundation Prerequisite | Observable Diagnostic Evidence | Diagnostic Gap Code | Immediate Pedagogical Response |

| **1** | **School Things Precision**<br>Differentiate `pen`, `pencil`, `rubber`, `ruler` in Unit 1 exercises | Disentangled lexical boundaries for stationery | Learner taps `pencil` when hearing *"Take out your blue pen"*; or types `pen` when shown a graphite pencil | `LEXICAL_BOUNDARY_CONFUSION`<br>`[PEN_VS_PENCIL]` | Deploy 2-card contrastive duel (`pen` ink vs `pencil` graphite); highlight visual affordance (writing tip); re-test in chunk *"blue pen"*. |
| **2** | **Textbook vs Notebook**<br>Follow teacher instruction: *"Open your book"* vs *"Write in your notebook"* | Semantic distinction between printed reading material and blank writing media | Learner taps textbook when asked to *"write in notebook"*; or hesitates >8s on book/notebook selection | `LEXICAL_BOUNDARY_CONFUSION`<br>`[BOOK_VS_NOTEBOOK]` | Visual contrast card: book (printed text, reading) vs notebook (empty ruled lines, writing); associate with verbs: `read a book` vs `write in a notebook`. |
| **3** | **Spoken Classroom Imperatives**<br>React to teacher classroom English (*"Open your book"*, *"Look at the board"*, *"Close your bag"*) | Fast auditory mapping from imperative verb chunks to physical action | Learner freezes; looks around at peers; waits for Vietnamese translation; reaction latency >10s | `RECEPTIVE_COMMAND_GAP`<br>`[IMPERATIVE_DECODING]` | Kinesthetic action drills (TPR-lite); audio-visual prompt with animated character demonstrating action; prompt fading over 3 cycles. |
| **4** | **School Verb Collocations**<br>Unit 1 A Closer Look 1: collocations with `play`, `do`, `have`, `study` | Categorical understanding of sports, activities, subjects, and possessions | Learner says *"play homework"* or *"do football"* or *"study uniform"* | `CHUNK_COLLOCATION_GAP`<br>`[VERB_NOUN_MISMATCH]` | 4-Quadrant sorting game: Sort items into `PLAY` (games/balls), `DO` (exercise/homework/judo), `HAVE` (objects/lunch), `STUDY` (academic subjects). |
| **5** | **Basic S-V-O Sentence Frame**<br>Getting Started dialogue: *"I have new books"*, *"We wear uniforms"* | Mental syntax rail preventing Vietnamese literal translation (*"Tôi có..." $\rightarrow$ "I have..."*) | Learner says *"I am new books"* (literal transfer of *Tôi là có...*) or *"I bag heavy"* (omitting verb) | `SYNTAX_ASSEMBLY_GAP`<br>`[S_V_O_SLOT_FAILURE]` | Tactile colored slot builder (Blue Subject, Orange Verb, Green Object); lockout slot preventing two verbs (`am have`); auditory playback of well-formed chunk. |
| **6** | **Third-Person Singular -s**<br>Unit 1 Grammar: Present Simple with *He/She/It* (*"Phong lives near here"*, *"He has a new bag"*) | Subject-verb agreement awareness and morphological inflection | Learner writes *"He study English"* or *"Phong have a bag"* | `MORPHOSYNTAX_GAP`<br>`[3RD_PERSON_AGREEMENT]` | Visual toggle switch on verb block: when subject changes from `I` to `Phong/He`, verb block glows and snaps on `-s / -es / has`. |
| **7** | **Final Consonant Articulation**<br>Unit 1 Pronunciation: Releasing word-final stops /k/, /t/, /d/, /p/ | Phonological muscle memory preventing L1 Vietnamese final consonant omission | Learner pronounces *"boo"* for `book`, *"ba"* for `bag`, *"wri"* for `write` | `PHONOLOGICAL_GAP`<br>`[FINAL_CONSONANT_DELETION]` | Visual articulatory cue ("Puff of air" / "Click sound"); audio waveform feedback displaying the missing burst energy; non-punitive re-prompt. |
| **8** | **Vowel Quality Contrast**<br>Unit 1 Pronunciation: /ɑː/ (smart, art) vs /ʌ/ (study, subject, rubber) | Auditory discrimination between long open /ɑː/ and short central /ʌ/ | Learner misidentifies minimal pairs; pronounces *"study"* as /stɑːdi/ or *"smart"* as /smʌrt/ | `PHONETIC_DISCRIMINATION_GAP`<br>`[VOWEL_LENGTH_QUALITY]` | Minimal pair listening radar; visual mouth aperture guide (Wide Open jaw vs Relaxed Mid jaw); immediate auditory echo playback. |
| **9** | **Reading School Schedules**<br>Extract information from school timetables and subject lists | Basic decoding of academic subject names (Maths, Science, English, History) | Learner unable to map written subject name to meaning; fails timetable puzzle | `WRITTEN_LEXICAL_FORM_GAP`<br>`[SUBJECT_NAMES]` | Subject icon-to-spelling matching; interactive timetable puzzle with subject icons; sound-form reinforcing flash cards. |
| **10** | **Personal Profile Creation**<br>Unit 1 Project: Introduce oneself, school things, and favorite subjects | Productive synthesis of vocabulary and simple sentence frames | Learner can answer multiple choice, but cannot produce any spoken or written sentence about themselves | `PRODUCTIVE_TRANSFER_GAP`<br>`[AUTONOMOUS_OUTPUT]` | Scaffolded Profile Badge (Mission D); slot-fill personalization allowing high freedom; safe rehearsal with optional audio recording. |

## 3. REAL-TIME GAP ESCALATION & ESCAPE HATCH PROTOCOLS

To ensure that neither false credit is awarded nor learner motivation destroyed:

```
                          [ LEARNER RESPONSE ]
                                   │
              ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
              ▼                                         ▼
     [ Target Retrieved ]                      [ Target Failed / Related ]
              │                                         │
              ▼                                         ▼
   { Verify Independency }                     { Classify Failure }
      - Prompted?                                 - Close semantic related? (e.g. pencil)
      - Latency < heuristic?                      - Random click?
              │                                   - Auditory confusion?
              ▼                                         │
    [ CONFIRMED PASS ]                                  ▼
                                               [ ACTIVE GAP LOGGED ]
                                                        │
                                                        ▼
                                            [ TARGETED BRIDGE LAUNCH ]
                                              (Max 120s micro-repair)
                                                        │
                                                        ▼
                                            [ RESUME MAIN SCHOOL MISSION ]
```

### The Non-Punitive Escalation Rule
1. **Never derail the entire 35-minute lesson for a single micro-gap**: A vocabulary gap triggers an immediate 60–90 second Level B bridge activity. Once the distinction is highlighted, the session returns to the main narrative mission.
2. **Spaced Ingestion into FSRS v5**: The identified gap (`active_gap`) is quietly queued into the student's personal review deck, surfacing naturally in the next session's Warm-up Phase without overwhelming the child today.
