# 23. N=1 Single-Learner Pilot Protocol

> **Curriculum Project**: English From Zero — Foundation Curriculum (Grade 6 Vietnam)  
> **Status**: P0.5 Reality Check Before N=1 Pilot (Conditionality Baseline)  
> **Target**: Single Grade 6 Learner (N=1), Age 11–12, Pre-A1 / High Foreign Language Anxiety  
> **Scope**: Diagnostic Baseline $\rightarrow$ Lessons 001–003 Execution $\rightarrow$ Immediate Probes $\rightarrow$ Delayed Probe  
> **Safeguard Standard**: Reasonable safeguards have been designed for a low-risk educational pilot. Participant emotional comfort and agency take absolute precedence over curriculum data collection.

---

## 1. Participant Screening & School Intake

### A. Inclusion Criteria
1. **Age & Grade**: Currently enrolled in Grade 6 (Lớp 6), aged 11–12.
2. **Linguistic Level**: Pre-A1 / Below A1 ("Mất gốc"). Cannot produce a spontaneous 3-word English sentence.
3. **Affective Profile**: Self-reports or parent-reports fear of making mistakes, low confidence, avoidance of English homework, or classroom anxiety when called upon.
4. **Language Environment**: Native Vietnamese speaker in Vietnam.

### B. Safeguards & Participant Assent
- Both parent and learner give informed assent in Vietnamese.
- Clear explanation to child: *"Đây không phải là một bài thi hay bài kiểm tra lấy điểm. Thầy cô chỉ muốn tìm cách giúp môn tiếng Anh trở nên nhẹ nhàng, vui vẻ và dễ dàng nhất cho con. Bất cứ lúc nào con thấy mệt hay ngại, con đều có quyền xin dừng lại."*
- Zero penalty, zero grading, zero public recording.

## 3. Step-by-Step Pilot Execution Protocol

### Step 0: Pre-Pilot Baseline Probe (Session 0 — 10 Minutes)
- **Objective**: Determine entry behavior without teaching the target items beforehand.
- **Probe Tasks**:
  1. *Warm Chat in Vietnamese (3 mins)*: Establish friendly rapport; ask about hobbies and games.
  2. *Receptive Picture Check (3 mins)*: Show 4 picture cards (*football, cat, run, book*). Say words; ask child to point.
  3. *Spontaneous Sentence Prompt (2 mins)*: Show soccer picture: *"Con có thể nói gì về bức tranh này bằng tiếng Anh không?"* Observe if learner produces words or pauses.
  4. *Learner Self-Report Baseline (2 mins)*: Present the visual experience scale:
     🙂 Dễ / ổn | 😐 Hơi khó | 🙁 Khó | 🛑 Con muốn dừng

---

### Step 1: Lesson 001 Execution (Session 1 — 30–35 Minutes)
- **Title**: **Mission 1: Survive Your First Day at School (Global Success Unit 1 Bridge)**
- **Pedagogical Alignment**: Replaced babyish puppet/sticker games with age-appropriate secondary school survival missions:
  1. **Mission A: School Bag Challenge**
     - *Level F*: Sound-to-image probe (`bag, pen, book, drink` with canonical V0 images).
     - *Rapid Upward Adaptation*: If learner solves 2 consecutive items independently, skip remaining trivial nouns immediately.
     - *Level B*: Lexical boundary contrasts (`pen` vs `pencil`, `book` vs `notebook`).
     - *Level X*: 30-second backpack optimization puzzle under 3.0 kg weight limit for Tuesday timetable (*Maths, English, Art*).
  2. **Mission B: Decode the Teacher (Classroom Commands)**
     - Real classroom imperatives: *"Open your book to page 10"*, *"Take out your pen and write down"*, *"Look at the board"*, *"Close your bag"*.
  3. **Mission C: The Language Puzzle (S-V-O Syntax)**
     - Color-coded syntax blocks (`[I] [have] [new books]`, `[We] [wear] [uniforms]`, `[I] [study] [English]`) to eradicate Vietnamese literal translation (*"I am new books"*).
  4. **Mission D: Craft Your School Identity Badge**
     - Creative profile creation and SafeHarbor speech check focusing on final consonant release (/k/ in *book*, /z/ in *pens*).
- **Immediate Can-Do Check (5 mins)**:
  - Learner reacts to 1 live classroom instruction, differentiates `pen` vs `pencil`, and introduces their school badge.

---

### Step 2: Lesson 002 Execution (Session 2 — 45 Minutes)
- **Instructional Focus**:
  - Positive preference: `I like [football / games / music]`.
  - Contrastive dislike: `I don't like [fish / math]`.
  - Question: `Do you like [Noun]?`
- **Immediate Can-Do Check (5 mins)**:
  - 2-turn preference exchange with picture cards.

---

### Step 3: Lesson 003 Execution (Session 3 — 45 Minutes)
- **Instructional Focus (Theme Skin Selected with Learner)**:
  - Choice of Skin: Gaming abilities, Sports, Real-life skills, or Superpowers.
  - Capability chunks: `I can [action]`, `I can't [action]`.
- **Immediate Can-Do Check (5 mins)**:
  - Action miming declaration.

---

### Step 4: Delayed Retrieval Check (Administered after delay)
- Measures unprompted recall without reteaching before the check:
  - Prompt 1: Self-introduction dialogue.
  - Prompt 2: Express 1 like and 1 dislike from picture cards.
  - Prompt 3: Express 1 capability and 1 inability.

## 4. Evidence-Based Advancement Framework (Two Dimensions)

Advancement is decoupled from facial expressions or arbitrary single percentages. The teacher evaluates two independent dimensions:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 DIMENSION A: LEARNING PERFORMANCE (PER CAN-DO)              │
│                                                                             │
│  [ INDEPENDENT ] : Produces target chunk accurately without auditory model. │
│  [ MODELLED ]    : Produces target immediately after teacher model.         │
│  [ PROMPTED ]    : Requires gestural, visual card, or binary choice prompt. │
│  [ NOT YET ]     : Cannot produce target despite two scaffolds.             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 DIMENSION B: LEARNER EXPERIENCE (SELF-REPORT)               │
│                                                                             │
│  🙂 Dễ / ổn      : Learner feels comfortable, ready to continue.           │
│  😐 Hơi khó      : Learner feels challenged but willing to proceed.         │
│  🙁 Khó          : Learner feels overloaded or confused.                    │
│  🛑 Con muốn dừng: Immediate halt condition. Session pauses.                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Synthesis Decision Options:

| Evidence Pattern | Meaning & Next Action |
|---|---|
| **ADVANCE** | Core Can-Dos achieved Independently or with Modelled ease; Learner self-report is 🙂 or 😐; no excessive frustration. $\rightarrow$ *Proceed to next lesson.* |
| **ADVANCE WITH REVIEW** | Can-Dos achieved with Prompts; chunks are retained but specific words hesitate; Learner self-report is 🙂 or 😐. $\rightarrow$ *Proceed to next lesson, embedding a 5-minute retrieval warm-up for the hesitant items.* |
| **REPEAT PART** | Target chunks require heavy prompting; Learner self-report is 🙁; confusion concentrated in one specific structure (e.g. *like* vs *don't like*). $\rightarrow$ *Re-run only the confused task using tactile cards and physical games before introducing new material.* |
| **SIMPLIFY** | Multiple Can-Dos are "Not Yet"; Learner self-report is 🙁 or 🛑. $\rightarrow$ *Halt progression. Drop active productive requirements down to 2 words; provide binary choices; investigate whether task pacing was too fast.* |

## 5. Post-Pilot Success Evaluation (N=1 Pattern Analysis)

Success in the N=1 pilot is determined by **observable evidence patterns**, not false numerical precision:
1. **Communicative Exchange**: Can the learner participate in a simple 2-turn dialogue (*"Hello, my name is Minh. What's your name?"*)?
2. **Structural Transfer**: Does the learner maintain English S-V-O word order when given novel picture prompts?
3. **Comprehensible Speech**: Is the learner's speech understood by the teacher in context, without requiring exaggerated stop bursts?
4. **Affective Trajectory**: Does the learner's self-reported experience move from 🙁/😐 toward 🙂 across sessions?
