# Visual Style & Pedagogical Art Direction Guide

> **Target Audience**: Vietnamese Grade 6 Students (Ages 11–12, False Beginners, Pre-A1)  
> **Course**: English From Zero – Foundation Curriculum  
> **Status**: Approved Reference Standard  
> **Scope**: All student-facing flashcards, diagnostic cards, and situational illustrations  

---

## 1. Core Pedagogical & Developmental Philosophy

Visual assets in this curriculum are not decorative clipart. They constitute a **primary pedagogical layer** designed to:
1. Form direct neural mappings between spoken English phonology and real-world semantic concepts without passing through Vietnamese translation.
2. Provide anxiety-free diagnostic assessment for false beginners who freeze when confronted with written grammar.
3. Validate concept comprehension independently of written reading decoding skills.

### The Grade 6 Psychological Balancing Act
An 11–12-year-old Vietnamese learner stands at a sensitive psychological crossroads:
- **Graduation into Secondary School (*Lên cấp 2*)**: They take pride in leaving primary school (*Tiểu học*).
- **Extreme Aversion to Infantilization**: If learning materials feature babyish chibi characters (3-head body proportions, gigantic toddler eyes, nursery rhyme aesthetics), students feel patronized, embarrassed, and disengaged (*"Cái này cho trẻ mẫu giáo, em lớn rồi"*).
- **Anxiety Around Adult Corporate Imagery**: Conversely, stock photos of adults in business suits, corporate boardrooms, or complex office workstations feel alien, emotionally detached, and intimidating.
- **The Design Sweet Spot: Modern Educational Illustration**:
  - Dignified, youthful, contemporary graphic novel / editorial vector aesthetic.
  - Natural adolescent anatomy (6 to 6.5 head proportions).
  - Clean linework with rich, friendly, warm flat-color palettes.
  - Respectful of the learner's developmental dignity.

## 2. Character Design & Cultural Context

### 2.1 Student Archetypes
- **Age**: Exactly 11–12 years old (early secondary school).
- **Proportions**: Realistic adolescent proportions (6.0–6.5 heads high). Slender, active, youthful silhouettes. Avoid hyper-muscular figures, adult curves, or stubby chibi toddlers.
- **Facial Features**: Expressive, friendly, natural Vietnamese features. Dark hair with modern, neat cuts; warm almond-shaped dark brown eyes; natural skin tones ranging from light olive to sun-kissed tan.
- **Attire & School Uniform**:
  - *Standard School Uniform*: Crisp white short-sleeved button-down shirt with a neatly folded red scarf (*khăn quàng đỏ*) tied at the collar; dark navy blue or black straight-cut trousers for boys/girls, or pleated dark navy knee-length skirts for girls. Clean white or dark sneakers.
  - *Physical Education (PE)*: Sporty breathable polo shirt with school color stripes, navy track pants, running shoes.
  - *Casual Wear*: Unbranded cotton t-shirts (soft pastel or primary colors: light blue, muted yellow, forest green), denim or khaki shorts/pants.

### 2.2 Adult Supporting Characters
- **Teachers**: Warm, approachable secondary school educators (aged 25–45). Neatly dressed in smart-casual collared shirts, blouses, or traditional graceful *áo dài* for ceremonial/school opening scenes.
- **Parents**: Relatable everyday Vietnamese mothers and fathers (aged 35–48) wearing comfortable domestic clothes (linen shirts, simple homewear).

### 2.3 Environmental Settings
- **Classroom**: Contemporary Vietnamese public secondary classroom: wooden desks with light steel frames arranged in pairs or rows; large chalkboard or green chalkboard at the front; ceiling fans; large open windows with natural daylight; concrete corridors.
- **Schoolyard**: Paved courtyard with red bricks or concrete, shade from mature *cây bàng* (tropical almond) or *cây phượng vĩ* (flame tree), bicycle/e-bike parking racks.
- **Home**: Modest, modern Vietnamese apartment or townhouse: light tiled floors, natural wooden dining table, clean kitchen with rice cooker and kettle, cozy student study corner with a focused desk lamp.

## 3. Visual Semantic Clarity & Object Isolation (The 70% Rule)

Diagnostic testing requires absolute visual unambiguity. If a learner hesitates, that hesitation must reflect linguistic processing—never visual confusion.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|  CANVAS MARGIN (15%)                                  |
|     +-------------------------------------------+     |
|     |  FOCAL OBJECT / ACTION ZONE (70%)         |     |
|     |                                           |     |
|     |   * Undisputed central subject            |     |
|     |   * Crisp silhouette & contrast           |     |
|     |   * High visual weight                    |     |
|     |                                           |     |
|     +-------------------------------------------+     |
|  MINIMAL SUBORDINATE BACKGROUND (15%)                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 The Golden 70% Focal Rule
- The target object or action must occupy **60% to 75%** of the central canvas area.
- The viewer’s eye must immediately lock onto the target within **500 milliseconds**.

### 3.2 Background Subordination
- Backgrounds must remain minimalist, muted, and low-contrast.
- Use soft pastel gradients, minimal architectural outlines, or subtle shadows.
- **Zero Distracting Props**: In a card for *pen*, do not place a notebook, laptop, coffee cup, and smartphone next to it. Render a single clean pen resting cleanly on a desk plane with a soft drop shadow.

### 3.3 Visual Contrast Pair Discipline
When testing related or antonymous concepts, the composition must establish unmistakable visual discrimination:
- **`run` vs `walk`**:
  - *Run*: Dynamic forward lean ($30^\circ$), both feet off the ground in flight phase, elbows bent at sharp angles, wind motion lines.
  - *Walk*: Upright spine, heel planted on ground while other foot rolls off toe, arms swinging gently.
- **`big` vs `small`**:
  - Always render two objects in the same frame sharing the same ground plane to establish relative scale (e.g. an oversized school backpack next to a tiny pencil case, with an explicit visual callout or comparative framing).
- **`hot` vs `cold`**:
  - *Hot*: Student wiping forehead sweat with hand, unbuttoned collar, sun overhead, handheld paper fan.
  - *Cold*: Student in warm padded puffer jacket, wool scarf, hands tucked in pockets, visible frosty breath.

## 4. Absolute Prohibition on Text Leakage (Zero-Text Standard)

To maintain assessment validity, **NO WRITTEN LANGUAGE MAY APPEAR WITHIN THE IMAGE**:

| Forbidden Artifact | Risk to Pedagogy | Required Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| English words on covers (e.g. "ENGLISH 6", "NOTEBOOK") | Learner reads English letters rather than retrieving vocabulary from memory | Solid plain color cover with a blank geometric design |
| Brand names (e.g. "NIKE", "THIÊN LONG", "VINAMILK") | Commercial distraction and brand-reading | Unbranded clean shapes with generic color blocking |
| Packaging labels (e.g. "MILK" on carton, "WATER" on bottle) | Answer leakage | Plain carton with a simple milk-drop icon; clear transparent water glass |
| Classroom posters with English slogans | Background noise / false reading cues | Abstract colored charts, geometry shapes, or clean blank boards |
| Text on t-shirts / clothing | Answer leakage or confusion | Solid monochrome or simple two-tone striped t-shirts |

## 5. Technical Specifications & Production Standards

### 5.1 Aspect Ratios & Resolutions
1. **Vocabulary Flashcards & Diagnostic Cards (1:1 Square)**:
   - Master Generation: $1024 \times 1024$ px (PNG).
   - Production Web Display: $800 \times 800$ px (WebP, quality 85).
   - Mobile Thumbnail Display: $400 \times 400$ px (WebP, quality 80).
2. **Contextual Situational Scenes & Dialogue Frames (16:9 Widescreen)**:
   - Master Generation: $1536 \times 864$ px (PNG).
   - Production Web Display: $1280 \times 720$ px (WebP, quality 85).

### 5.2 Performance & File Size Budgets
- **Vocabulary Flashcard WebP**: $\le 75\text{ KB}$ (Target: $45\text{--}65\text{ KB}$).
- **Context Scene WebP**: $\le 140\text{ KB}$ (Target: $90\text{--}120\text{ KB}$).
- Fast hydration requirement: Four diagnostic cards must download concurrently in $< 200\text{ms}$ over 4G mobile connections.

### 5.3 Color Palette & Accessibility
- **Primary Line Work**: Deep charcoal/navy (`#1E293B`), not harsh pure black (`#000000`), ensuring warm organic appeal.
- **Base Background**: Neutral warm off-white (`#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`) ensuring high contrast in both dark and light UI containers.
- **Semantic Accents**:
  - Warm energetic tones: Amber (`#F59E0B`), Coral (`#F43F5E`) for action and warmth.
  - Calm educational tones: Sky Blue (`#0EA5E9`), Mint Green (`#10B981`) for objects and school settings.
- **Accessibility**: All key subject contours must meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratio ($\ge 3:1$ against the card background).

## 6. Prompt Engineering Formula (16 Required Fields)

Every visual asset generated for the curriculum must be formulated using this standardized specification:

```markdown
### [IMAGE_ID]: [TARGET_WORD]

1. IMAGE_ID: IMG-G6-XXX
2. TARGET_CONCEPT: The exact lemma or chunk (e.g., 'bag', 'swim', 'hot')
3. USE_CASE: Flashcard / Diagnostic Test / Contextual Storyboard
4. AGE_CONTEXT: 11–12-year-old Vietnamese secondary school learner (Grade 6)
5. SCENE: Brief situational context
6. SUBJECT: Primary focal subject (object, character, or action)
7. ACTION: Explicit dynamic posture or movement
8. COMPOSITION: Subject position, scale (60–75%), framing
9. BACKGROUND: Minimalist, soft, non-competing elements
10. VISUAL_STYLE: Modern educational illustration, clean semi-flat vector, gentle gradients, charcoal line art
11. CAMERA / FRAMING: Eye-level / slight high-angle, central framing, sharp focus
12. LIGHTING: Bright, clean, cheerful, warm natural daylight, soft contact shadows
13. DISTRACTOR_RESTRICTIONS: Specific secondary objects strictly forbidden
14. TEXT_RESTRICTIONS: Zero letters, zero numbers, zero typography, zero symbols
15. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS: photorealistic 35mm, chibi, 3D Pixar doll, hyper-detailed clutter, dark gloomy mood, text, watermark
16. ASPECT_RATIO: 1:1 (Square) or 16:9 (Widescreen)
17. PEDAGOGICAL_REASON: Why this visual framing guarantees zero ambiguity for false beginners
```
