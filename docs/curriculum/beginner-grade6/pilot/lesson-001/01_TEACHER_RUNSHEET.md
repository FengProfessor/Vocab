# Lesson 001 Teacher Runsheet (Quick 1-Page Field Guide)

> **Unit**: Lesson 001 — Hello & Names  
> **Target Learner**: 11–12-year-old Grade 6 Vietnamese false beginner  
> **Session Length**: 40–45 minutes (Flexible)  
> **Core Ethos**: Low anxiety, high communicative success, zero judging.

---

### 1. LESSON GOAL & TODAY'S CAN-DO
- **Lesson Goal**: Experience a positive, low-anxiety first English interaction.
- **Today's Can-Do**: *"I can introduce myself (name + age) and ask someone's name in a simple 2-way dialogue."*

---

### 2. TARGET LANGUAGE & CHUNKS
- **Greetings**: `Hello!`, `Bye!`
- **Identity Chunk 1**: `My name is [Name].`
- **Identity Chunk 2**: `I'm [11 / 12].` *(Accept "I'm 12" directly; do NOT force "years old")*
- **Question Chunk**: `What's your name?`

---

### 3. MATERIALS NEEDED
- 1 soft small ball (or funny plush toy).
- Name card sticker for student and teacher.
- 4 large word cards: `[Hello!]`, `[My name is...]`, `[I'm 12]`, `[What's your name?]`.
- Student Sheet (`02_STUDENT_MATERIAL.md`).

---

### 4. MINUTE-BY-MINUTE INSTRUCTIONAL SEQUENCE

| Time | What Teacher Shows | What Teacher Says | What Student Does | Optional Vietnamese Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0–8m**<br>Warm Welcome | Teacher smiles, waves, wears own name sticker: "Alex". | *"Hello! Welcome!"* (Vẫy tay cười) | Waves back, smiles, relaxes. | *"Hôm nay không kiểm tra, không chấm điểm gì cả. Cứ thoải mái cùng thầy/cô nhé!"* |
| **8–18m**<br>Avatar Story | Shows Avatar picture on phone/card: "Nam, 12". | *"Hello! My name is Nam. I'm 12. What's your name?"* (Play/Say 2x) | Listens, points to Nam, nods. | *"Bạn ấy bảo bạn ấy tên gì nhỉ? Bao nhiêu tuổi?"* $\rightarrow$ Học sinh trả lời tiếng Việt. |
| **18–26m**<br>Mouth Gym *(Optional)* | Shows word card `[name]`. Demonstrates gentle lip closing. | *"Look at my lips: na... me. /neɪm/."* | Closes lips at end of word. | *(Optional cue)*: *"Ngậm môi nhẹ ở chữ name nhé con."* (Bỏ qua nếu con thấy ngại). |
| **26–36m**<br>Card Assembly | Puts 3 cards on table: `[Hello!]`, `[My name is...]`, `[What's your name?]`. | Models placing own name: *"My name is Alex."* | Student places their own name card. Points and speaks. | Cho con tự chọn thứ tự ghép thẻ. |
| **36–42m**<br>Ball Toss Dialogue | Teacher holds soft ball, rolls gently to student. | *"Hello! My name is Alex. (Rolls ball) What's your name?"* | Student catches ball: *"My name is [Minh]. (Rolls back) What's your name?"* | Thầy/cô làm mẫu lượt đầu trước để con bắt chước dễ dàng. |
| **42–45m**<br>Victory Win | Hands student the "Identity Master" sticker. | *"High five! You did real English today!"* | High-fives, smiles, completes self-report. | *"Hôm nay con đã nói chuyện tiếng Anh 2 chiều như người lớn rồi đấy!"* |

---

### 5. TEACHER ACTION PROTOCOLS

- **WHAT NOT TO CORRECT**:
  - Do NOT correct if student drops "years old" (saying *"I'm 12"* is completely natural English!).
  - Do NOT demand loud, explosive consonant releases.
  - Do NOT correct grammar terminology or ask "tại sao dùng is".
- **IF STUDENT STRUGGLES (FREEZES OR HESITATES >10s)**:
  - Immediately give binary choice: *"Con nói 'Minh' hay 'Nam' nào?"* $\rightarrow$ Let student echo.
  - Switch to non-verbal pointing first, then choral speech (speak together).
- **IF STUDENT MOVES FAST**:
  - Add school name or feeling: *"I'm happy today!"* or *"I'm a student at Nguyen Du."*
- **STOP CONDITION**:
  - If the student shows acute distress (crying, verbal withdrawal, points to 🛑 on self-report), **HALT IMMEDIATELY**. Switch 100% to Vietnamese, offer water, talk about games/pets, and end session warmly.
- **END-OF-LESSON MICRO-WIN**:
  - Student and teacher perform the 2-way dialogue once smoothly:  
    *Student*: "Hello! My name is Minh. What's your name?"  
    *Teacher*: "My name is Alex. Nice to meet you, Minh!"  
    *High five!*
