# Lesson 001 Delayed Retrieval Check (3–5 Minutes)

> **Administration Timing**: At the start of the next meeting (48–72 hours after Lesson 001).  
> **Critical Rule**: Administer this check **BEFORE** opening any textbooks, flashcards, or review games. Do NOT pre-teach or hint before probing.

---

### Step 1: Warm Casual Greeting
- Teacher smiles and waves: *"Hello!"*
- Does student respond with an English greeting automatically?
  - `[ ] Responds "Hello" / "Hi" spontaneously`
  - `[ ] Responds in Vietnamese`
  - `[ ] Waves silently`

---

### Step 2: Unprompted Self-Introduction Probe
- Teacher holds a small toy or gestures to self:  
  *"Hello! My name is Alex. (Chờ 5 giây xem học sinh có tự nói không. Nếu không, hỏi:) What's your name?"*

- **Target 1: Can-Do Name (`My name is [Name]`)**:
  - `[ ] Independent` (Produces *"My name is..."* without model)
  - `[ ] Modelled` (Echoes after teacher re-models)
  - `[ ] Prompted` (Says name only, e.g. "Minh", then completes after gesture)
  - `[ ] Not yet` (Cannot retrieve chunk)

- **Target 2: Can-Do Question (`What's your name?`)**:
  - Does the student ask the teacher's name back?
  - `[ ] Spontaneous question asked back`
  - `[ ] Question asked with gesture prompt ("Còn con hỏi thầy/cô đi?")`
  - `[ ] Not yet`

- **Target 3: Can-Do Age (`I'm [Age]`)**:
  - Teacher asks: *"How old are you?"*
  - `[ ] Says "I'm 12" (or 11)`
  - `[ ] Says number only ("12")`
  - `[ ] Not yet`

---

### Step 3: Learner Experience Check
- *"Sau vài ngày, con nhớ lại bài trước thấy thế nào?"*
  - `[ ] 🙂 Vẫn nhớ & thấy dễ`
  - `[ ] 😐 Hơi quên chút nhưng nhớ lại rồi`
  - `[ ] 🙁 Quên hết rồi ạ`

---

### Step 4: Outcome & Transition
- Immediate high-five! Regardless of result, praise effort.
- Transition immediately to planned review or Lesson 002.
