## 1. The Dual-Track Integration Architecture

To solve the tension between remedial recovery and immediate school survival, LingoPro implements two synchronized tracks:

```
[TRACK 1: FOUNDATION TRACK (Stages 0–5, Lessons 001–036)]
- Primary Purpose: Systematic reconstruction of phonology, high-utility chunks, and S-V-O syntax.
- Atmosphere: 100% low-stakes, zero exam pressure, high self-efficacy.
- Progress Gate: Student must complete Stage 3 before attempting school bridge tasks.
                         │
                         ▼ (Upon achieving Stage 3 Exit Gate)
[TRACK 2: SCHOOL BRIDGE TRACK (Stage 6, Lessons 037–042 + Parallel Modules)]
- Primary Purpose: Direct mapping to MOET Global Success Tiếng Anh 6 Units 1–3.
- Atmosphere: Demystifying textbook reading passages, classroom tests, and board work.
- Outcome: Student walks into school English class feeling competent and prepared.
```

## 3. Handling the Classroom Test: The 15-Minute Exam De-Escalation

The single biggest trigger of school panic for Vietnamese 6th graders is the unannounced **"Kiểm tra 15 phút"** or regular 45-minute tests.

In **Lesson 041**, we train specific test-taking coping mechanisms:
1. **The Question-Stem Decoders**:
   - Teach learners to recognize instructions instantly without panic:
     - `Choose the correct answer` $\rightarrow$ Chọn đáp án đúng.
     - `Find the mistake` $\rightarrow$ Tìm lỗi sai (thường là thiếu đuôi -s hoặc to be).
     - `Rearrange the words` $\rightarrow$ Xếp lại câu theo thứ tự $S - V - O$.
2. **The "Spot the Missing Verb" Strategy**:
   - If an option has no verb, it cannot be a sentence.
3. **The Breathing Reset**:
   - A 5-second physical pause before starting the test: inhale deeply, smile, remind oneself: *"Con đã luyện phản xạ ở LingoPro rồi, bài này con làm được!"*
