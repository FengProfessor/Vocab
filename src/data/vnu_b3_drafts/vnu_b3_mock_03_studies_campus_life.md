## PHẦN 1: TƯƠNG TÁC XÃ HỘI (PART 1 — SOCIAL INTERACTION: ~3 PHÚT)

*Giám khảo (hoặc máy tính) đọc lần lượt từng câu hỏi. Thí sinh phản xạ trực tiếp trong khoảng 25–35 giây cho mỗi câu hỏi.*

### Chủ đề A: University Major & Academic Life (Chuyên ngành đại học & Môn học yêu thích)

#### Câu hỏi 1: What academic major are you currently studying at university?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Hiện tại tiếp diễn diễn tả quá trình học (*am studying, am majoring in*) và tên chuyên ngành.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Currently, I am a third-year student majoring in Information Technology at the University of Engineering and Technology, VNU. In my program, we study fundamental computer science subjects such as software engineering, database management, and computer networking. Although the curriculum is quite demanding, I find it fascinating."

#### Câu hỏi 2: Why did you choose this field of study?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc nêu lý do và mục đích (*choose... because, in order to, offer career opportunities*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "I chose this field primarily because I have had a strong passion for computers and coding since I was in high school. Furthermore, the technology industry in Vietnam is booming rapidly, which means there are plentiful job opportunities with attractive salaries for qualified graduates in this sector."

#### Câu hỏi 3: Which subject do you find the most interesting or rewarding?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc so sánh bậc nhất (*the most interesting, the most rewarding*) và mệnh đề quan hệ (*which helps me*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Among all the courses in my curriculum, I find Web Development to be the most rewarding subject. In this class, our lecturer teaches us how to build responsive websites from scratch using modern frameworks. Seeing my own interactive code work on the screen gives me a tremendous sense of accomplishment."

### Chủ đề B: Study Habits & Campus Environment (Thói quen tự học & Không gian giảng đường)

#### Câu hỏi 4: Do you prefer studying alone in a library or in a study group with classmates?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc lựa chọn so sánh (*prefer A to B / prefer V-ing rather than V-ing*) và đối chiếu ưu nhược điểm.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Well, it depends on the task at hand. When I need to understand complex theories or prepare for written exams, I prefer studying alone in a quiet corner of the library to maintain deep concentration. However, when working on team assignments, gathering with classmates allows us to brainstorm diverse ideas effectively."

#### Câu hỏi 5: What is your favorite spot on your university campus?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Giới từ nơi chốn, tính từ miêu tả không gian học đường và cảm xúc cá nhân.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "My favorite spot on campus is definitely the university central library garden. It is shaded by large green trees and furnished with wooden study benches. During lunch breaks, my classmates and I often sit there with iced milk tea to relax, read books, and discuss lecture topics under the cool breeze."

#### Câu hỏi 6: How are you going to prepare for your final exams this semester?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Tương lai gần (*be going to + V*) và các giải pháp chuẩn bị học tập.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "To prepare for the upcoming final exams, I am going to make a detailed revision timetable. I plan to review all lecture slides, summarize key concepts into mind maps, and practice solving past exam papers. Additionally, I am going to form a study group with my friends to quiz each other before the test."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: A Memorable University Group Project (Dự án học tập nhóm đáng nhớ)

```
Describe a memorable school or university project you participated in.
You should say:
• What the project was about
• Who you collaborated with in your team
• What challenges you and your team faced
And explain what you learned from working on this project.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Project:* Built a mobile food-delivery app prototype for Marketing & IT course, last semester.
- *Team:* 4 classmates (2 coders, 2 market researchers).
- *Challenges:* Clashing opinions, tight 3-week deadline, laptop crashed before final presentation.
- *Learned:* Teamwork communication, active listening, dividing tasks clearly based on strengths.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to talk about a memorable group project that I participated in during my third semester at university. We were assigned to develop a prototype for a mobile food-delivery application in our e-commerce course.
> 
> Our team consisted of four members, including two programmers and two marketing researchers. At the beginning, we faced considerable difficulties because of conflicting opinions regarding the interface design and user experience. Moreover, we had a very tight deadline of only three weeks to submit our final report.
> 
> To overcome these hurdles, we held daily online meetings every evening to delegate tasks based on each member's core strengths. While our teammates were conducting survey questionnaires with students, I stayed up late writing code for the login system. In the end, on presentation day, our team delivered a smooth live demo and earned an A grade from our professor.
> 
> Through this project, I learned invaluable lessons about teamwork and patience. I realized that clear communication and mutual respect are far more crucial than individual technical skills."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 2: Overcoming a Difficult Exam (Vượt qua một kỳ thi đầy thử thách)

```
Describe a time you found an exam or assignment very difficult but overcame it.
You should say:
• What exam or subject it was and when it happened
• Why it was so challenging for you
• How you prepared and what actions you took to pass
And explain how you felt when you received your final result.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Exam & When:* Advanced Calculus / Higher Mathematics, first year at university.
- *Why challenging:* Abstract formulas, fast-paced professor lectures, failed midterm test (only 4.0/10).
- *Actions taken:* Woke up 5:00 AM daily, did practice tests, asked senior tutors in library for help, watched online tutorials.
- *Feelings:* Received 8.0/10; immense relief, gained confidence that hard work beats fear.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to share an unforgettable experience from my first year at university when I had to overcome an extremely challenging exam in Advanced Calculus.
> 
> In our first semester, mathematics was a nightmare for me because the lectures were very fast-paced and the theoretical proofs were deeply abstract. In fact, I felt devastated when I received only 4 out of 10 points on my midterm test. I was genuinely terrified of failing the entire course and having to retake it.
> 
> Instead of giving up, I decided to take drastic action. For three consecutive weeks, I woke up at 5:00 AM every morning to solve calculus exercises. Whenever I encountered an insurmountable formula, I consulted senior tutors at the academic support center in our library. Furthermore, I watched YouTube video tutorials repeatedly until I understood the core principles.
> 
> Finally, when the exam results were published on the portal, I was overjoyed to see that I had achieved 8 out of 10. That moment gave me immense confidence that dedication and discipline can overcome any academic barrier."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Present Tenses, Relative Clauses & Future Plans)
* **Hiện tại tiếp diễn miêu tả tiến trình học hiện tại:**
  - $S + am/is/are + V-ing$: *"I am currently majoring in Software Engineering."* Lưu ý không nhầm với hành động nhất thời.
* **Mệnh đề quan hệ xác định (Defining Relative Clauses) với *Which / That*:**
  - Dùng để giải thích tính chất ngành học mà không bị ngắt câu: *"It is a major which provides extensive practical skills"* hoặc *"Web development is a subject that helps me create interactive products"*.
* **Kế hoạch tương lai với *Be Going To*:**
  - $S + am/is/are + going \ to + V_0$: *"I am going to make a revision timetable and practice past papers."*

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Past Continuous + Past Simple, Sequence & Phrasal Verbs)
* **Quá khứ tiếp diễn kết hợp Quá khứ đơn (*While / When*):**
  - Diễn tả hành động đang diễn ra trong quá trình học thì một trở ngại phát sinh:
    - *"While we were conducting survey questionnaires, our laptop battery suddenly died."*
    - *"While I was preparing for the final exam, I felt immense anxiety."*
* **Cụm động từ (Phrasal Verbs) học đường thông dụng:**
  - `burn the midnight oil` (thức khuya học bài)
  - `hand in / submit` (nộp bài tập)
  - `catch up with` (theo kịp tiến độ bài học)
  - `give up` (từ bỏ, buông xuôi)
  - `pass with flying colors` (vượt qua kỳ thi với điểm số rực rỡ)
* **Liên từ chỉ nguyên nhân - kết quả:**
  - *Because of conflicting opinions $\to$ Therefore, we held daily meetings.*
  - *Instead of giving up $\to$ I decided to take drastic action.*

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách khắc phục (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi dùng "major in" sai giới từ:** Học sinh hay dùng *"major on"* hoặc *"major at"* $\to$ Luôn luôn là *"major in [subject]"*.
2. **Lỗi phát âm đuôi -ed của động từ học đường:**
   - *passed* $\to$ `/pɑːst/` (âm `/t/`, không đọc là `/pɑː-sɪd/`).
   - *graduated* $\to$ `/ˈɡrædʒ.u.eɪ.tɪd/` (âm `/ɪd/`).
3. **Lỗi dùng "fail in the exam":** Trong tiếng Anh chuẩn, nói *"fail the exam"* (không có giới từ *in*).

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
- *Nếu học sinh không học đại học (thí sinh tự do hoặc người đi làm):* Giáo viên chuyển câu hỏi sang trải nghiệm thời cấp 3 hoặc khóa học tiếng Anh hiện tại:
  - *"What did you study in high school?"* hoặc *"What is your current occupation?"*
- *Nếu học sinh sợ câu hỏi về môn học khó:*
  - *Thay vì hỏi:* "How did you overcome cognitive bottlenecks in academic calculus?"
  - *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Did you ever get a bad mark in an exam? What did you do to get a better mark next time?"

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
- Giới thiệu ngành học: *"To begin with, I am in my [first/second/third] year at [university name], majoring in..."*
- Giải thích lý do chọn ngành: *"The primary reason I selected this major is that..."*
- Kể chuyện thi cử (Part 2): *"I still remember the hardest exam I ever sat, which was [subject name] during my [first] semester..."*
- Bài học đọng lại: *"What I took away from that challenge was that persistence always pays off."*
