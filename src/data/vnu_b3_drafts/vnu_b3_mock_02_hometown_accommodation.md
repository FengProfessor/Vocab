## PHẦN 1: TƯƠNG TÁC XÃ HỘI (PART 1 — SOCIAL INTERACTION: ~3 PHÚT)

*Giám khảo (hoặc hệ thống máy tính) đọc lần lượt từng câu hỏi. Thí sinh phản xạ trực tiếp trong khoảng 25–35 giây cho mỗi câu hỏi.*

### Chủ đề A: Your Hometown (Quê hương & Nơi chôn rau cắt rốn)

#### Câu hỏi 1: Where is your hometown located, and what is it famous for?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Giới từ chỉ phương hướng/vị trí (*in the north of, located in*) và cấu trúc *famous for / renowned for*.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Well, my hometown is Nam Dinh province, which is located in the northern part of Vietnam, about ninety kilometers south of Hanoi. It is widely renowned for its traditional beef noodle soup, known as Pho Bo Nam Dinh, as well as historic Tran Temple. Whenever tourists visit my hometown, they are always impressed by the friendly locals and delicious local cuisine."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What do you like most about living in or visiting your hometown?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Tính từ miêu tả môi trường sống (*peaceful, serene, fresh*) và mệnh đề nguyên nhân (*because, as*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "What I adore most about my hometown is the tranquil and slow-paced atmosphere. Unlike the bustling life and heavy traffic in Hanoi, my hometown has plenty of open green spaces and fresh air. Whenever I return home on vacations, I can walk along quiet village roads and enjoy homemade meals with my family, which relieves all my stress."

#### Câu hỏi 3: Has your hometown changed much over the past few years?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (*has changed, have been built*) kết hợp đối chiếu Hiện tại vs Quá khứ.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Yes, absolutely. Over the past five years, my hometown has undergone significant modernization. In the past, there were mainly narrow soil paths and old tiled houses. But nowadays, wide asphalt roads, new industrial zones, and modern convenience stores have replaced them. While this brings better job opportunities, it has also made the town slightly noisier than before."

---

### Chủ đề B: Living Accommodation & Rooms (Nơi ở & Không gian phòng ốc)

#### Câu hỏi 4: Do you currently live in a house or in an apartment?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Từ vựng nhà ở (*rented apartment, shared house, dormitory*) và mô tả vị trí.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "At present, because I am studying at university in Hanoi, I live in a shared rented apartment with two classmates near Cau Giay district. It is located on the sixth floor of a residential building. Living here is extremely convenient because it is within walking distance of my university campus and several bus stops."

#### Câu hỏi 5: Which room in your home is your favorite, and why?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc *favorite room*, tính từ miêu tả nội thất và công năng của không gian.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Without a doubt, my own bedroom is my favorite sanctuary. It is quite small but very cozy and full of natural sunlight from a large window. In my room, I have a wooden study desk, a small bookshelf, and a potted succulent. It is the only place where I can study in complete silence and enjoy a peaceful night's sleep."

#### Câu hỏi 6: Would you like to move to a different city or house in the future?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc ước muốn / dự định tương lai (*would like to + V, hope to, intend to*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Yes, in the future, once I graduate and establish a stable career, I would definitely love to buy a modern apartment in Da Nang city. I have always dreamed of living near the sea because coastal cities offer cleaner air and a much higher quality of life compared to crowded metropolises."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: Moving to a New Accommodation (Trải nghiệm chuyển chỗ ở mới)

```
Describe a time you moved to a new house, room, or dormitory.
You should say:
• When and where you moved
• Why you had to move to this new accommodation
• What you did on the moving day
And explain how you felt about your new living space.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *When & Where:* September last year, moved from old dorm to rented flat in Cau Giay.
- *Why moved:* Old dorm was overcrowded (8 students in 1 room), noisy, lacked private study space.
- *Moving day:* Packed clothes into cardboard boxes, hired a motorbike truck, carried boxes up 4 floors, unpacked till midnight.
- *Feelings:* Physically exhausted, but thrilled; felt independent, spacious, quiet room.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to describe a memorable experience when I moved into a new rented room in Hanoi in September last year, right before my sophomore year began. 
> 
> Previously, I lived in the university dormitory. Although it was cheap, eight students had to share a cramped room, which made it impossible for me to concentrate on studying at night. Therefore, my close friend and I decided to rent a private room in an apartment building in Cau Giay district.
> 
> On the moving day, we woke up at 6:00 AM to pack our clothes, textbooks, and cooking utensils into cardboard boxes. We hired a three-wheel motorbike truck to transport our belongings across the city. Carrying heavy boxes up to the fourth floor without an elevator was exhausting, and we were sweating profusely. Afterwards, we spent the entire afternoon cleaning the floor and arranging our furniture.
> 
> When everything was finally set up, I felt an immense sense of relief and happiness. The new room was much brighter, quieter, and more comfortable than our old dorm. It gave me the personal space I desperately needed to focus on my studies."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 2: Visiting a Friend's or Relative's Home (Chuyến thăm nhà người quen đáng nhớ)

```
Describe a memorable visit to a friend's or relative's home.
You should say:
• Whose home you visited and where it was located
• What the house looked like
• What activities you did together during the visit
And explain why this visit was particularly memorable to you.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Whose home & Where:* High school best friend's house in Moc Chau (Son La), last summer holiday.
- *Looked like:* Traditional wooden stilt house on a hillside, surrounded by green tea hills and plum orchards.
- *Activities:* Picked fresh plums, cooked local chicken hotpot with bamboo shoots, chatted on the wooden porch under starry sky.
- *Why memorable:* Warm hospitality of his family, breathtaking countryside scenery, unforgettable cultural experience.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~160 từ):
> "Today, I would like to tell you about a memorable trip to visit my high school best friend's family house in Moc Chau, which is a mountainous district in Son La province. 
> 
> I visited him during the summer vacation last year. His family lived in a traditional wooden stilt house located on a gentle hillside, completely surrounded by lush green tea hills. The architecture was fascinating, made entirely of dark natural wood with a large open veranda that overlooked the valley.
> 
> During my three-day stay, my friend's parents treated me like their own child. In the morning, we helped them pick ripe plums in the orchard behind the house. In the evening, the family prepared a delicious local hotpot featuring free-range chicken and fresh mountain herbs. After dinner, we sat together on the wooden porch, sipping hot green tea and stargazing.
> 
> That visit was truly unforgettable because of the genuine warmth and hospitality of his family. It was much cozier than any commercial hotel, and it allowed me to deeply appreciate rural Vietnamese life."

### CÂU HỎI MỞ RỘNG (FURTHER QUESTIONS: ~1.5 PHÚT)

#### Câu hỏi 1: Is it better for young adults to live with their parents or rent an apartment independently? Why?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "In my opinion, both options have distinct advantages, but renting independently is better for personal development. Living with parents saves a great deal of money on food and rent. However, living independently forces young adults to learn essential life skills such as budgeting, cooking, and solving household problems on their own. Therefore, it makes them mature much faster."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What features will modern houses have in smart cities of the future?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "I believe future houses will be heavily integrated with artificial intelligence and green energy technologies. For example, lights, air conditioners, and security locks will be operated automatically through voice commands or smartphones. Furthermore, most homes will probably install solar panels on their roofs to generate clean electricity and reduce environmental footprints."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Present Simple, Prepositions of Place & There is/are)
* **Cấu trúc *There is* và *There are*:**
  - *There is + singular noun / uncountable noun:* *There is a small bookshelf; There is plenty of fresh air.*
  - *There are + plural noun:* *There are wide asphalt roads; There are several bus stops near my flat.*
* **Giới từ chỉ vị trí và khoảng cách (Prepositions of Place & Distance):**
  - Giới từ địa lý: *in the north of Vietnam* (khác với *on the northern border*).
  - Tầng nhà: *on the sixth floor* (luôn dùng giới từ `on`).
  - Cụm diễn đạt tiện ích: *within walking distance of* (trong khoảng cách đi bộ), *in the vicinity of* (ở vùng lân cận).
* **Diễn đạt ước muốn tương lai (*Would like to* vs *Hope to*):**
  - Cấu trúc: $S + would \ like + to-V$ thể hiện mong muốn lịch sự, nhã nhặn hơn $want + to-V$. Ví dụ: *"I would definitely love to buy an apartment in Da Nang."*

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Past Simple, Adjectives & Comparatives)
* **Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple):**
  - Chuỗi hành động dồn dập trong ngày dọn nhà: *woke up, packed, hired, carried, unpacked, spent, felt*.
  - Lưu ý các cặp động từ: *lend / rent / hire* (Học sinh hay nhầm *hire a truck* với *borrow a truck*).
* **Trật tự tính từ miêu tả nhà cửa (OSASCOMP):**
  - **O**pinion $\to$ **S**ize $\to$ **A**ge $\to$ **S**hape $\to$ **C**olor $\to$ **O**rigin $\to$ **M**aterial $\to$ **P**urpose.
  - Ví dụ chuẩn B1: *a traditional wooden stilt house* (Opinion/Age + Material + Noun); *a cozy small bedroom* (Opinion + Size + Noun).
* **Cấu trúc so sánh hơn (Comparative forms):**
  - Tính từ ngắn: *cleaner, brighter, cozier* ($Adj-er + than$).
  - Tính từ dài: *more comfortable, more spacious, more independent* ($more + Adj + than$).
  - Trạng từ tăng cấp (Emphasizers): Dùng `much` hoặc `significantly` đứng trước để tạo độ tự nhiên: *"It was much brighter and significantly quieter than our old dorm."*

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách khắc phục (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi dùng sai giới từ tầng nhà:** Học sinh hay nói *"in the 6th floor"* $\to$ Chuẩn ngữ pháp phải là *"on the 6th floor"*.
2. **Lỗi dùng "crowded of":** Học sinh hay nói *"crowded of people"* $\to$ Đúng phải là *"crowded with people"* hoặc *"overcrowded"*.
3. **Lỗi quên mạo từ khi miêu tả phòng:** *"I have wooden desk"* $\to$ Phải có mạo từ: *"I have a wooden desk"*.

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
- *Thay vì hỏi:* "How has the architectural landscape of your hometown evolved?"
- *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Are there more tall buildings and roads in your hometown now than when you were a child?"
- *Thay vì hỏi:* "What are the socio-economic implications of independent student living?"
- *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Do you like living with roommates or living with your parents? Why?"

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
- Miêu tả quê hương: *"To tell you the truth, my hometown is located in [province], which is famous for [food/landmark]..."*
- Miêu tả chỗ ở: *"Currently, I am living in a [rented room/dormitory/house] together with [roommates/family]..."*
- Kể chuyện dọn nhà (Part 2): *"I still remember vividly when I had to move to a new place last year..."*
- So sánh chỗ ở cũ và mới: *"Compared to my previous room, the new one is much more..."*
