### Chủ đề A: Smartphone & Mobile App Habits (Thói quen dùng điện thoại & Ứng dụng di động)

#### Câu hỏi 1: How much time do you spend using your smartphone every day?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc *spend time on / spend time doing something* và ước lượng thời gian hàng ngày.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "On average, I spend approximately four to five hours a day on my smartphone. A significant portion of that screen time is dedicated to looking up academic materials, checking university notifications, and listening to English podcasts. However, I must admit that I also spend about an hour every night scrolling through short videos for relaxation."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What mobile applications do you open most frequently?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Từ vựng ứng dụng di động (*messaging, social networking, e-learning*) và trạng từ tần suất.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "The applications I open most frequently are Zalo, YouTube, and Canvas. I use Zalo throughout the day to communicate with my study groups and family members. Meanwhile, YouTube is my favorite platform for watching educational tutorials, and Canvas is where I submit homework assignments to my university lecturers."

#### Câu hỏi 3: What is the most useful feature of your smartphone for your daily routine?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc so sánh bậc nhất (*the most useful feature*) và mệnh đề chỉ công năng (*allows me to, enables me to*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "In my opinion, the most indispensable feature is the digital alarm and calendar reminder. Because I have a very packed schedule consisting of lectures, club activities, and part-time shifts, the calendar notification ensures that I never miss an important deadline or arrive late for classes."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: A Tech Breakdown Incident (Sự cố thiết bị công nghệ bị hỏng)

```
Describe a time when a technological device (phone/laptop) broke down or caused you trouble.
You should say:
• What device broke down and when it happened
• What important task you were doing when it malfunctioned
• How you tried to fix the problem
And explain how you felt and what you learned from this experience.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *What device & When:* Old Asus laptop, last semester, midnight before deadline.
- *What task:* Typing final 10-page research paper for Sociology class, due 8:00 AM next morning.
- *Problem & Fix:* Screen suddenly turned blue (blue screen of death), froze; panicked; rebooted, tried recovery mode, lost 3 unsaved pages; borrowed roommate's laptop to retype.
- *Feelings & Lesson:* Terrified, stressed; learned to always backup files on Google Drive and enable auto-save.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to talk about a terrifying incident when my laptop broke down during the final exam week last semester. 
> 
> It happened around midnight on a Sunday, while I was rushing to complete a ten-page research paper for my sociology course, which was due at eight o'clock the following morning. Suddenly, without any warning, the screen froze and turned completely blue. The keyboard was unresponsive, and the cooling fan made a loud buzzing noise.
> 
> I was in a state of absolute panic because I had not saved the last three pages. I desperately tried to restart the machine multiple times, but it kept displaying a hard drive error. Knowing that I had no time to lose, I woke up my roommate and asked to borrow his laptop. I stayed up until five in the morning rewriting the missing paragraphs from memory.
> 
> Although I managed to submit the paper just before the deadline, that night was pure agony. That stressful experience taught me a vital lesson: always save documents onto cloud storage like Google Drive and enable auto-save continuously."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Duration, Zero/First Conditional & Modals)
* **Cấu trúc đo lường thời lượng:**
  - $Spend + time + (on \ N / in \ V-ing)$: *"I spend approximately four to five hours a day on my smartphone."*
  - $It \ takes + someone + time + to-V$: *"It only takes me two minutes to check emails."*
* **Câu điều kiện loại 0 & 1 (Conditionals):**
  - Loại 0 (thói quen): *"If I get a notification, I usually check it immediately."*
  - Loại 1 (hệ quả tương lai): *"If I spend too much time scrolling through TikTok, I will fail to meet my study deadlines."*
* **Động từ khuyết thiếu khả năng (*Allow / Enable*):**
  - $allow / enable + someone + to-V$: *"The calendar notification enables me to manage my schedule without missing deadlines."*

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Past Continuous, Tech Incident Verbs & Comparatives)
* **Quá khứ tiếp diễn kết hợp Quá khứ đơn (*While / Suddenly*):**
  - *"While I was rushing to complete a ten-page paper, the screen froze."*
  - Diễn tả sự gián đoạn đột ngột (interrupted action) của một tiến trình quan trọng.
* **Động từ chuyên biệt về sự cố kỹ thuật (Technical Breakdown Verbs):**
  - `freeze` $\to$ `froze` (đóng băng, đơ máy)
  - `crash` $\to$ `crashed` (sập nguồn, lỗi phần mềm)
  - `reboot / restart` (khởi động lại)
  - `malfunction` $\to$ `malfunctioned` (hoạt động sai chức năng)
  - `synchronize seamlessly` (đồng bộ hóa mượt mà)
* **So sánh hơn tính từ và trạng từ (Comparatives):**
  - *significantly more convenient than*
  - *much more effectively than*
  - *weaken genuine relationships*

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách khắc phục (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi dùng "spend time to browse":** Sau *spend time* luôn là danh từ hoặc $V-ing$ (*spend time browsing*), không dùng *to-V*.
2. **Lỗi dùng "social network" vs "social media":**
   - *Social network* nhấn mạnh mạng lưới kết nối (Facebook, Zalo).
   - *Social media* nhấn mạnh phương tiện truyền thông nội dung.
   - Tránh nói *"I play social network"* $\to$ Hãy nói *"I use / browse social media"*.
3. **Lỗi quên chia quá khứ của động từ bất quy tắc:** *freeze* $\to$ *froze* (không phải *freezed*); *lose* $\to$ *lost* (không phải *losed*).

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
- *Nếu học sinh không rành về thuật ngữ công nghệ phức tạp:*
  - *Thay vì hỏi:* "How does ubiquitous mobile computing transform your interpersonal communication?"
  - *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Do you talk to your friends on Facebook or in person more often? Why?"
- *Nếu học sinh chưa từng bị hỏng laptop nặng:*
  - Hướng dẫn học sinh kể về sự cố nhẹ: Điện thoại bị hết pin (battery died), quên mật khẩu (forgot password), hoặc mất kết nối Wi-Fi khi đang làm bài.

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
- Nói về ứng dụng yêu thích: *"When it comes to my mobile usage, the app that I rely on most is..."*
- Nói về thói quen nhắn tin: *"To be honest, I am definitely more of a texting person because..."*
- Kể chuyện hỏng thiết bị (Part 2): *"I still shudder when I recall the time my [phone/laptop] crashed right before..."*
- Kể về ứng dụng mới: *"A couple of months ago, a friend introduced me to a fantastic app called..."*
- Bàn về tương lai công nghệ: *"Looking ahead to the next ten years, I am convinced that..."*
