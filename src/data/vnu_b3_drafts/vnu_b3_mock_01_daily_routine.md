## PHẦN 1: TƯƠNG TÁC XÃ HỘI (PART 1 — SOCIAL INTERACTION: ~3 PHÚT)

*Giám khảo (hoặc hệ thống khảo thí máy tính) đọc lần lượt từng câu hỏi. Thí sinh phản xạ trực tiếp trong khoảng 25–35 giây cho mỗi câu hỏi mà không có thời gian chuẩn bị trước.*

### Chủ đề A: Morning Routine & Daily Schedule (Lịch trình buổi sáng & Thói quen ngày thường)

#### Câu hỏi 1: What time do you usually wake up on weekdays?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Khả năng dùng thì Hiện tại đơn, chỉ giờ giấc và trạng từ chỉ tần suất.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Well, on weekdays, my alarm clock usually goes off at 6:30 in the morning. I try to get out of bed immediately because I have to attend morning lectures at university at 7:30. However, if I stay up late the night before to finish assignments, I might sleep in until 7:00 and grab a quick breakfast on the way."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What is the first thing you typically do after getting out of bed?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Liên từ chỉ chuỗi hành động (*first, then, after that*) và các cụm động từ thường nhật.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "To be honest, the very first thing I do is drink a large glass of warm water to wake up my body. After that, I wash my face, brush my teeth, and do some light stretching for about five minutes. Doing this helps me feel refreshed and completely ready for a busy study day."

#### Câu hỏi 3: Which part of the day do you feel most productive and energetic?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** So sánh các khoảng thời gian trong ngày và giải thích lý do (*because, since*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "I definitely feel most energetic and productive in the early morning, especially between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM. During these hours, my mind is completely clear and the surroundings are relatively quiet, so I can concentrate deeply on solving difficult mathematics exercises or reading academic papers."

### Chủ đề B: Leisure Time & Weekend Activities (Thời gian rảnh rỗi & Hoạt động cuối tuần)

#### Câu hỏi 4: What leisure activities do you enjoy doing during your free time?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Động từ chỉ sở thích (*enjoy, love, prefer*) đi kèm danh động từ (*V-ing*) và từ vựng giải trí.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "In my spare time, I really enjoy listening to acoustic pop music and reading detective novels. Whenever I feel exhausted after class, putting on my earphones and listening to my favorite playlist helps me relieve stress significantly. Sometimes, I also play badminton with my neighbors in the late afternoon."

#### Câu hỏi 5: Do you prefer spending your weekends alone or hanging out with friends?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc so sánh sở thích (*prefer A to B / prefer doing A rather than B*) và lý do bổ trợ.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Actually, it depends on my energy level during that week. Most of the time, I prefer hanging out with two or three close friends at a cozy coffee shop because we can chat, share funny stories, and catch up with each other. But if the previous week was too demanding, I choose to stay at home alone to recharge my batteries."

#### Câu hỏi 6: What are you going to do this coming weekend to relax?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Tương lai gần (*be going to + V*) để diễn tả kế hoạch/dự định đã lên lịch trước.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "This coming Saturday, I am going to have a picnic with my university classmates at West Lake. We have already planned to rent bicycles to cycle around the lake in the late afternoon and then enjoy some traditional street food together. I am really looking forward to it."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: A Memorable Weekend Activity (Trải nghiệm thư giãn cuối tuần đáng nhớ)

```
Describe a memorable weekend activity you enjoyed recently.
You should say:
• What activity you did and where you went
• Who you spent that weekend with
• What you did during the day
And explain why that weekend was so enjoyable and memorable to you.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *What & Where:* Camping trip, Ba Vi National Park, 2 weeks ago.
- *Who:* 3 university roommates (Nam, Phong, Lan).
- *Activities:* Rented a tent, grilled BBQ chicken, sang acoustic songs, watched sunrise.
- *Why memorable:* Escaped city noise, strengthened friendship, refreshed mind before exam week.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~160 từ):
> "Today, I would like to talk about a wonderful weekend trip that I enjoyed about two weeks ago. My university roommates and I decided to go camping at Ba Vi National Park, which is about sixty kilometers from central Hanoi.
> 
> We set off early on Saturday morning by motorbike. When we arrived, the weather was exceptionally pleasant, with fresh cool air and green pine trees everywhere. First, we found a flat grassy area near the camping site to pitch our tent. After that, while the boys were gathering dry wood to build a campfire, the girls prepared food and marinated meat. In the evening, we grilled chicken, sat together around the fire, and sang acoustic songs. 
> 
> What made this weekend so memorable to me was that we completely disconnected from smartphones and social media. It was much more relaxing than staying in our cramped dormitory room. Looking back, that trip not only helped us relieve academic stress before our final exams, but it also made our friendship much stronger."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 2: A Busy & Stressful Day (Một ngày bận rộn và nhiều áp lực)

```
Describe a time you had a very busy and stressful day.
You should say:
• When this day happened and why it was so busy
• What tasks or duties you had to complete
• How you managed to finish everything
And explain how you felt at the end of that exhausting day.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *When & Why:* Last month, midterm exam + part-time job deadline on the same day.
- *Tasks:* 8:00 AM finished English exam; 1:00 PM gave presentation in economics; 6:00 PM worked shift at coffee shop.
- *How managed:* Made a strict to-do list, drank coffee, prioritized urgent tasks, stayed focused.
- *Feelings:* Completely exhausted, but proud of perseverance and time management.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to share an experience about one of the busiest and most stressful days I had last month during our university midterm week. On that particular Friday, I had to deal with two major academic deadlines as well as my part-time working shift.
> 
> My day began at 6:00 AM. In the morning, I took an important English test at 8:00 AM. Immediately after finishing the test, I rushed to the library to review my slides because our team had a group presentation in business economics at 1:30 PM. To make matters worse, while we were presenting, the projector suddenly malfunctioned, which made me feel extremely nervous. However, we managed to solve it and received a compliment from our lecturer. In the evening, from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM, I had to work as a barista at a busy coffee shop.
> 
> By the time I returned to my room at 10:30 PM, I was completely drained and exhausted. Nevertheless, I felt extremely proud of myself because I managed to complete every single responsibility without giving up."

- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "Yes, I definitely agree with that statement. In modern society, people are under immense pressure from work competition and fast-paced lifestyles. In contrast, people in the past lived more simply and had clearer boundaries between work and family time."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Present Simple & Future Forms)
* **Hiện tại đơn (Present Simple):**
  - *Form:* Khẳng định: $S + V_{(s/es)}$; Phủ định: $S + do/does + not + V_0$; Nghi vấn: $Do/Does + S + V_0?$
  - *Meaning & Use:* Dùng để diễn tả thói quen lặp đi lặp lại hàng ngày (*I wake up at 6:30*), chân lý hoặc sự thật ổn định (*My lecture starts at 7:30*).
  - *Trạng từ chỉ tần suất (Adverbs of Frequency):* `always`, `usually`, `frequently`, `sometimes`, `hardly ever`, `never`. Lưu ý vị trí: đứng trước động từ thường (*I usually drink water*) và đứng sau động từ to be (*I am always on time*).
* **Tương lai với *Be Going To* vs *Will*:**
  - *Be going to + V:* Dùng cho dự định cuối tuần đã có sự chuẩn bị hoặc sắp đặt trước (*We are going to have a picnic; we have already rented bicycles*).
  - *Will + V:* Dùng cho phỏng đoán tại thời điểm nói hoặc suy đoán cá nhân (*I think I will probably stay home*).

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Past Simple, Past Continuous & Sequence Markers)
* **Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple):**
  - *Form:* Động từ có quy tắc thêm `-ed` (*decided, enjoyed, arrived*); Động từ bất quy tắc biến đổi (*went, woke, set, sat, sang, felt, took*).
  - *Quy tắc phát âm đuôi -ed:*
    - Phát âm là `/t/` sau các âm vô thanh: `/p/, /k/, /f/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/` (ví dụ: *rushed* `/rʌʃt/`, *cooked* `/kʊkt/`).
    - Phát âm là `/ɪd/` sau âm `/t/` và `/d/` (ví dụ: *decided* `/dɪˈsaɪ.dɪd/`, *marinated* `/ˈmær.ɪ.neɪ.tɪd/`).
    - Phát âm là `/d/` cho các trường hợp còn lại (ví dụ: *enjoyed* `/ɪnˈdʒɔɪd/`, *arrived* `/əˈraɪvd/`).
* **Quá khứ tiếp diễn kết hợp Quá khứ đơn (Past Continuous + Past Simple):**
  - *Cấu trúc:* $S + was/were + V-ing \quad \text{while / when} \quad S + V_{2/ed}$.
  - *Tác dụng sư phạm:* Tạo bối cảnh nền sống động cho câu chuyện. Ví dụ: *"While the boys were gathering dry wood, the girls prepared food"* hoặc *"While we were presenting, the projector suddenly malfunctioned"*.
* **Từ nối tiến trình thời gian (Sequence Markers):**
  - `First / To begin with` $\to$ `Then / Next / After that` $\to$ `Suddenly / At that moment` $\to$ `In the end / Finally / Looking back`.

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách phòng tránh (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi trôi tuột về Hiện tại đơn (Tense Slippage):** Học sinh bắt đầu bằng *"Two weeks ago I went..."* nhưng câu tiếp theo lại nói *"Then we go to Ba Vi and we eat BBQ"*.  
   $\to$ *Giải pháp:* Trước khi nói Part 2, viết to chữ **PAST (-ED)** lên đầu tờ giấy nháp để tự nhắc nhở bản thân.
2. **Lỗi quên chia ngôi thứ 3 số ít:** *"My alarm clock go off"* $\to$ Phải là *"My alarm clock goes off"*.
3. **Lỗi trả lời cộc lốc ở Part 1:** Chỉ trả lời *"6:30 AM"* hoặc *"Acoustic music"*.  
   $\to$ *Giải pháp:* Áp dụng triệt để công thức AREA (Answer trực tiếp $\to$ Reason giải thích $\to$ Example mở rộng).

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
Nếu học sinh gặp hiện tượng "đơ não" (3-second cognitive freeze) khi nghe câu hỏi trừu tượng, giáo viên/AI tutor chẻ nhỏ câu hỏi:
- *Thay vì hỏi:* "Which part of the day do you feel most productive and energetic?"
- *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Do you feel more energetic in the morning or in the evening? Why?"
- *Thay vì hỏi:* "Do you think modern citizens suffer from time poverty?"
- *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Are you usually very busy with schoolwork? Do you wish you had more free time on weekends?"

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
*Dành cho học sinh sợ sai ngữ pháp dùng để khởi động luồng nói:*
- Mở đầu Part 1: *"Well, to be honest, on normal weekdays, I usually..."*
- Nêu lý do Part 1: *"The main reason is that it helps me stay energetic throughout the day."*
- Mở đầu Part 2: *"Today, I would like to talk about a memorable experience that took place about [two weeks ago / last month]..."*
- Chuyển ý Part 2: *"After that, we decided to..."* | *"What impressed me the most was that..."*
- Kết luận cảm xúc: *"Looking back, that experience taught me that..."*

### 4. Túi câu đệm chống "im lặng chết" (Anti-Freezing Time-Buying Fillers)
Khi chưa kịp nảy số ý tưởng, học sinh sử dụng ngay các cụm từ này để kéo dài 2–3 giây suy nghĩ thay vì im lặng:
- *"Well, let me see... That's quite an interesting question."*
- *"To be perfectly honest, I haven't thought about that before, but I would say that..."*
- *"Actually, in my personal routine, I tend to..."*
- *"If I had to choose between the two, I would definitely go for..."*
