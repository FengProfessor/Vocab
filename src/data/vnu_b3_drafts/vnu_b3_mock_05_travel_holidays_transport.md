## PHẦN 1: TƯƠNG TÁC XÃ HỘI (PART 1 — SOCIAL INTERACTION: ~3 PHÚT)

*Giám khảo (hoặc máy tính) đọc lần lượt từng câu hỏi. Thí sinh phản xạ trực tiếp trong khoảng 25–35 giây cho mỗi câu hỏi.*

### Chủ đề A: Holiday Preferences & Travel Habits (Sở thích kỳ nghỉ & Thói quen du lịch)

#### Câu hỏi 1: Do you prefer going on vacation to the beach or to the mountains?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc so sánh sở thích (*prefer A to B*) và từ vựng miêu tả phong cảnh tự nhiên.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Personally, I definitely prefer heading to the beach for a holiday. I love the feeling of walking barefoot on soft white sand, listening to the soothing ocean waves, and swimming in the cool blue sea. Breathing fresh salty sea air helps me clear my mind much more effectively than climbing steep mountain paths."

#### Câu hỏi 2: Who do you usually travel with: your family or your friends?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** So sánh đối tượng đồng hành và tính chất của chuyến đi (*family trip vs trip with friends*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "It depends on the purpose of the trip. During major holidays like Tet, I always travel with my family because it strengthens our bonds and creates cherished memories with my parents. However, during summer breaks, I prefer going on adventure trips with close friends because we can participate in energetic outdoor sports and stay up late together."

#### Câu hỏi 3: How often do you travel to a new destination each year?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Trạng từ và cụm từ chỉ tần suất (*once or twice a year, whenever I have free time*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Because I am a busy university student on a limited budget, I usually travel to a brand new destination about once or twice a year, mainly during the summer vacation or right after finishing final exams. Exploring a new city allows me to taste local cuisine and broaden my horizons beyond the capital."

### Chủ đề B: Transportation & Commuting (Phương tiện di chuyển & Giao thông thường nhật)

#### Câu hỏi 4: What means of transport do you use most frequently every day?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Giới từ chỉ phương tiện (*go by motorbike, commute by bus*) và lý do lựa chọn.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "On a daily basis, my primary means of transportation is my motorbike. In a dense city like Hanoi, riding a motorbike is extremely flexible because I can easily navigate through small alleys and avoid heavy traffic jams during rush hours. It only takes me fifteen minutes to get to my campus."

#### Câu hỏi 5: What do you like or dislike about public transport in your city?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Đưa ra quan điểm tích cực và tiêu cực về phương tiện công cộng (*inexpensive, eco-friendly vs crowded, delayed*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "What I like most about the public bus system in Hanoi is that it is remarkably cheap, costing only about eight thousand dong per ride, and the vehicles are fully air-conditioned. On the other hand, the biggest drawback is that buses often arrive late due to road congestion, and they can be suffocatingly crowded during peak morning hours."

#### Câu hỏi 6: Where will you travel for your next summer holiday?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Tương lai đơn (*will travel*) hoặc Tương lai gần (*be planning to / be going to*) chỉ kế hoạch.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "For my next summer holiday, I am planning to visit Da Nang and Hoi An Ancient Town with my university classmates. We have already started saving pocket money and looking for budget flight tickets. I can hardly wait to stroll along the lantern-lit streets of Hoi An and taste authentic Cao Lau noodles."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: An Unforgettable Vacation (Kỳ nghỉ đáng nhớ trong quá khứ)

```
Describe a memorable vacation or trip you took in the past.
You should say:
• Where you went and when you went there
• Who accompanied you on the journey
• What activities you engaged in during the trip
And explain why this vacation was so special and unforgettable to you.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Where & When:* Phu Quoc Island, 4-day trip, last summer after freshman year.
- *Who:* 3 close high school friends.
- *Activities:* Rented motorbikes, visited Sao Beach, snorkeled to see coral reefs, tasted fresh sea urchin at Night Market.
- *Why memorable:* First time traveling far without parents, breathtaking sunset, deepened long-lasting friendship.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to talk about an extraordinary four-day trip to Phu Quoc Island that I took with three of my closest high school friends last July. It was our first major journey traveling independently without our parents.
> 
> We took a direct two-hour flight from Hanoi to Phu Quoc. Upon arriving at the island, we were stunned by the crystal-clear turquoise water and swaying coconut palms. First, we rented motorbikes to cruise around the island. One afternoon, while we were taking a boat tour around An Thoi archipelago, we went snorkeling and marveled at the colorful coral reefs beneath the surface. In the evenings, we strolled through Dinh Cau Night Market, tasting grilled sea urchin and fresh seafood skewers.
> 
> What made this holiday truly unforgettable was the incredible feeling of freedom and maturity. Watching the dramatic orange sunset over the Gulf of Thailand while talking about our future dreams made us feel deeply connected. It was undeniably the most magical vacation of my youth."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 2: A Travel Delay or Transportation Problem (Sự cố giao thông hoặc chậm trễ chuyến đi)

```
Describe a time you experienced a travel delay or transportation problem.
You should say:
• Where you were going and what transport you were using
• What caused the delay or problem
• What you did while waiting to resolve the issue
And explain how the incident affected your overall trip.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Where & Transport:* Train from Hanoi to Da Nang, Reunification Express, 2 years ago.
- *Cause:* Torrential rain and landslide near Central Vietnam triggered 5-hour delay in Hue.
- *What did while waiting:* Sat in waiting lounge, played card games with fellow passengers, shared snacks, read book.
- *How affected:* Missed hotel booking time, initially annoyed; but learned patience and made new friends on the journey.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to describe a rather stressful travel incident that occurred two summers ago when my family was traveling from Hanoi to Da Nang by passenger train.
> 
> Everything was progressing smoothly until our train approached the central region near Hue. Suddenly, the train came to a complete halt because torrential monsoon rains had triggered a severe landslide on the tracks ahead. The conductor announced over the loudspeaker that we had to wait at least five hours for maintenance crews to clear the debris.
> 
> At first, the entire cabin was filled with frustration and restlessness because the air conditioner inside the train started to weaken. However, instead of complaining endlessly, my siblings and I decided to buy instant noodles and share snacks with nearby passengers. We played card games and listened to fascinating stories told by an elderly local couple.
> 
> Although the five-hour delay meant that we missed our scheduled hotel check-in in Da Nang, the experience taught me how to stay calm and adaptable when unexpected challenges arise during travel."

### CÂU HỎI MỞ RỘNG (FURTHER QUESTIONS: ~1.5 PHÚT)

#### Câu hỏi 1: How has traveling become easier and more convenient compared to twenty years ago?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "Compared to twenty years ago, traveling has become substantially more accessible thanks to digital technology and budget airlines. In the past, people had to queue up at ticketing offices and rely on paper maps. Nowadays, travelers can book cheap flight tickets, reserve hotel rooms, and navigate foreign cities instantly using smartphone applications like Google Maps and Booking.com."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What negative impacts can excessive tourism have on local cultures and environments?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "Excessive tourism, or overtourism, often causes serious environmental degradation, such as plastic littering on beaches and damage to delicate marine ecosystems. Furthermore, rapid commercialization can erode local traditional identities, as historical neighborhoods are transformed into noisy souvenir shops and westernized bars designed solely for foreign tourists."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Prepositions of Transport, Adverbs & Future)
* **Giới từ chỉ phương tiện giao thông (Prepositions of Transport):**
  - Dùng `by + vehicle` (không có mạo từ): *by bus, by train, by car, by plane, by motorbike*.
  - Dùng `in` cho phương tiện cá nhân nhỏ: *in a car, in a taxi*.
  - Dùng `on` cho phương tiện công cộng lớn hoặc xe 2 bánh: *on the bus, on the train, on a motorbike, on a bicycle, on foot*.
* **Tương lai đơn vs Dự định (*Will* vs *Be planning to*):**
  - *I will probably visit...* (dự đoán chưa chắc chắn 100%).
  - *We are planning to visit / We are going to visit...* (đã có kế hoạch và bắt đầu tiết kiệm tiền).

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Past Continuous, Narrative Tenses & Superlatives)
* **Quá khứ tiếp diễn kết hợp Quá khứ đơn (Past Continuous + Past Simple):**
  - Miêu tả hành động nền đang diễn ra thì sự cố hoặc hành động nổi bật ập đến:
    - *"While we were taking a boat tour, we went snorkeling."*
    - *"While our train was approaching Hue, torrential rains triggered a landslide."*
* **Cấu trúc so sánh hơn & So sánh bậc nhất (Comparatives & Superlatives):**
  - *much more effectively than* (so sánh hơn trạng từ).
  - *undeniably the most magical vacation* (so sánh bậc nhất tính từ).
  - *substantially more accessible* (so sánh hơn tính từ với trạng từ bổ trợ).
* **Từ nối chỉ tiến trình sự kiện (Sequence Markers):**
  - `At first / To begin with` $\to$ `Suddenly / Out of the blue` $\to$ `Instead of [V-ing]` $\to$ `Although [Clause], in the end...`

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách khắc phục (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi dùng "go by foot":** Học sinh rất hay nói *"I go by foot"* $\to$ Chuẩn tiếng Anh phải là *"I go on foot"* hoặc *"I walk"*.
2. **Lỗi dùng "travel alone without parents":** Học sinh hay dịch thô *"travel alone with friends"* (mâu thuẫn từ nghĩa). Hãy dùng *"travel independently with friends"*.
3. **Lỗi phát âm tên địa danh quốc tế và Việt Nam:** Khi nói các địa danh như *Phu Quoc, Da Nang, Hoi An*, cần nói rõ ràng, tự nhiên, không gượng gạo; các từ tiếng Anh đi kèm như *Island, Ancient Town* phải phát âm chuẩn âm đuôi (`/ˈaɪ.lənd/`, `/taʊn/`).

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
- *Nếu học sinh chưa từng đi máy bay hoặc đi du lịch xa:*
  - Giáo viên hướng dẫn học sinh nói về một chuyến đi dã ngoại gần trường, một lần về quê bằng xe buýt hoặc chuyến cắm trại ở công viên: *"Tell me about a time you went to a park or visited your grandparents."*
- *Nếu học sinh bị bí từ khi miêu tả phương tiện giao thông:*
  - *Thay vì hỏi:* "What are the systemic deficiencies of urban transit infrastructure?"
  - *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "Do you take the bus or ride a motorbike? Is the bus cheap and comfortable?"

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
- Bắt đầu nói về sở thích du lịch: *"If you ask me about my travel preferences, I would say that..."*
- Miêu tả phương tiện đi lại: *"On a daily basis, the easiest way for me to get around the city is by..."*
- Mở đầu kể chuyện chuyến đi (Part 2): *"I would love to share a memorable holiday that I took [last summer / two years ago]..."*
- Kể về sự cố chuyến đi: *"Things didn't go as planned when suddenly..."*
- Kết luận cảm xúc: *"In spite of the minor delay, what stayed with me was..."*
