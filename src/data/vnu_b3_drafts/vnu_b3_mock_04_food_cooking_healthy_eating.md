## PHẦN 1: TƯƠNG TÁC XÃ HỘI (PART 1 — SOCIAL INTERACTION: ~3 PHÚT)

*Giám khảo (hoặc máy tính) đọc lần lượt từng câu hỏi. Thí sinh phản xạ trực tiếp trong khoảng 25–35 giây cho mỗi câu hỏi.*

### Chủ đề A: Favorite Food & Daily Meals (Món ăn yêu thích & Bữa ăn hàng ngày)

#### Câu hỏi 1: What is your favorite traditional Vietnamese dish?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Từ vựng ẩm thực, mệnh đề quan hệ giải thích món ăn và tính từ cảm giác.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Without a doubt, my absolute favorite dish is traditional Vietnamese Pho Bo, or beef noodle soup. It consists of flat rice noodles, tender slices of beef, and a savory broth simmered with star anise, cinnamon, and ginger for several hours. Whenever I eat a steaming bowl of Pho with fresh herbs and lime juice, it immediately comforts my stomach."

#### Câu hỏi 2: Do you prefer eating home-cooked meals or dining out at restaurants?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc so sánh sở thích (*prefer V-ing to V-ing*) và đối chiếu lợi ích dinh dưỡng vs sự tiện lợi.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Most of the time, I definitely prefer eating home-cooked meals. Preparing food at home allows me to select fresh organic ingredients and control the amount of cooking oil and salt. Eating out at restaurants is occasionally convenient when I hang out with friends, but home food is undeniably healthier and much cheaper."

#### Câu hỏi 3: What do you normally have for breakfast on a busy morning?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Trạng từ tần suất (*normally, usually*) và chuỗi thức ăn nhanh buổi sáng.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "On busy weekday mornings, I normally buy a traditional Vietnamese Banh Mi from a street vendor near my bus stop. It is a crispy baguette stuffed with pate, grilled pork, cucumber, and coriander. It only takes me two minutes to buy, and it provides me with enough carbohydrates and protein to stay energetic until lunchtime."

### Chủ đề B: Cooking Habits & Nutrition (Thói quen nấu nướng & Dinh dưỡng)

#### Câu hỏi 4: Can you cook, and how often do you prepare meals for yourself or your family?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Trợ động từ khả năng (*can cook*), trạng từ tần suất và các thao tác nấu nướng cơ bản.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Yes, I can cook quite decently. Because I am a university student living independently away from home, I prepare meals for myself about four or five times a week. I usually cook simple balanced dishes such as boiled vegetables, fried eggs, and tomato braised tofu. Cooking my own dinner is a relaxing way to unwind after long study hours."

#### Câu hỏi 5: What is the most important factor when you choose food: taste or nutrition?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Cấu trúc cân nhắc yếu tố cốt lõi (*prioritize, the most critical factor is...*).
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "If I have to choose, I would say nutritional value is the most important factor for me. While delicious taste gives instant pleasure, eating clean, balanced food with plenty of green vegetables and lean meat maintains my immune system and helps me stay mentally alert throughout the day."

#### Câu hỏi 6: Are you going to learn how to cook any new recipes in the near future?
- **Mục tiêu khảo thí:** Thì Tương lai gần (*be going to + V*) chỉ kế hoạch học hỏi kỹ năng mới.
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1 — AREA):**
  > "Yes, definitely. This coming summer vacation, I am going to ask my mother to teach me how to make traditional spring rolls, or Nem Ran. It is a delicate recipe that requires wrapping minced pork, mushrooms, and glass noodles neatly in rice paper before frying them to golden crispness."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 1: A Memorable Shared Meal (Bữa ăn sum họp ấm cúng và đáng nhớ)

```
Describe a memorable meal you shared with your family or friends.
You should say:
• Where and when you had this meal
• Who was there with you
• What delicious food was served
And explain why this meal made such a lasting impression on you.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *Where & When:* Lunar New Year's Eve (Tet) dinner, grandparents' ancestral home in Nam Dinh.
- *Who:* Extended family (grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, 6 cousins).
- *Dishes:* Square sticky rice cake (Banh Chung), boiled free-range chicken with lime leaves, bamboo shoot soup, caramelized pork with eggs.
- *Why memorable:* Cozy reunion, rarely gather all together, shared jokes and childhood memories, felt warmth of family bond.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~165 từ):
> "I would like to describe a truly heartwarming meal that I shared with my entire extended family on Lunar New Year's Eve last February at my grandparents' home in the countryside.
> 
> That evening, more than fifteen family members, including my aunts, uncles, and cousins from different cities, gathered together. The dining table was covered with authentic traditional Tet specialties. We had fragrant square sticky rice cake, golden boiled free-range chicken garnished with thinly sliced lime leaves, and a steaming bowl of dried bamboo shoot soup.
> 
> While we were enjoying the feast, my grandfather opened a bottle of homemade sticky rice wine and toasted to everyone's health and prosperity. The room was filled with joyous laughter as my relatives recounted funny childhood stories and congratulated each other on their achievements.
> 
> What made this meal leave such a deep impression on me was the profound sense of togetherness. In our fast-paced modern life, we are rarely able to gather under one roof. That dinner reminded me that family love is the most precious treasure."

### CUE-CARD OPTION 2: Trying Unfamiliar Food (Lần đầu nếm thử một món ăn lạ)

```
Describe a time you tried an exotic or unfamiliar food for the first time.
You should say:
• What food it was and where you tried it
• Why you decided to try it
• How it tasted and how you felt eating it
And explain whether you would recommend this dish to others.
```

#### Dàn ý 1 phút chuẩn bị (Candidate's 1-Minute Note-taking Outline)
- *What & Where:* Bun Dau Mam Tom (fermented shrimp paste tofu noodles), during first week in Hanoi as freshman.
- *Why:* Classmates invited, wanted to experience Hanoi specialty despite strong pungent smell.
- *Taste & Feelings:* Initially hesitant and nervous because of pungent smell; tasted crispy tofu dipped in shrimp paste with kumquat and chili; amazingly rich and savory flavor.
- *Recommend:* Definitely recommend to daring food lovers; authentic taste of Vietnamese street cuisine.

#### Bài nói mẫu B1 hoàn chỉnh (Model Storytelling Response — ~160 từ):
> "Today, I would like to talk about the first time I tried a rather unique Vietnamese dish called Bun Dau Mam Tom, which is rice vermicelli served with fried tofu and fermented shrimp paste. 
> 
> This happened during my first week as a university freshman in Hanoi. My new roommates took me to a well-known street food stall in an old alley near Hang Khay. At first, when the waiter brought the tray, I felt quite intimidated because the fermented shrimp paste had an extremely pungent and strong odor. I was honestly worried that my stomach would not tolerate it.
> 
> However, my roommates encouraged me to squeeze some fresh kumquat juice and mix it thoroughly with chili and sugar until it foamed up. I took a piece of golden crispy tofu, dipped it into the sauce, and took a bite. To my surprise, the combination was extraordinarily savory, crispy on the outside and velvety inside.
> 
> I would definitely recommend this dish to anyone seeking an authentic Vietnamese culinary adventure, provided they are brave enough to overcome the initial smell."

### CÂU HỎI MỞ RỘNG (FURTHER QUESTIONS: ~1.5 PHÚT)

#### Câu hỏi 1: Why is fast food becoming so popular among young people despite its health risks?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "In my view, fast food is thriving among youth mainly because of its extreme convenience, aggressive advertising, and addictive flavors. Modern students and young workers have hectic schedules, so grabbing a hamburger or fried chicken takes only five minutes. Furthermore, fast food chains provide air-conditioned spaces with free Wi-Fi, making them attractive meeting spots for youngsters."

#### Câu hỏi 2: What measures should schools take to promote healthy eating habits among students?
- **Model Answer (Chuẩn B1):**
  > "Schools should take concrete actions to educate students on nutrition. First, school canteens ought to replace sugary soft drinks and deep-fried snacks with fresh fruit, milk, and balanced lunches. Second, teachers can incorporate basic cooking and dietary workshops into biology or physical education classes so that children understand the dangers of obesity and diabetes early on."

## GHI CHÚ NGỮ PHÁP CỐT LÕI (GRAMMAR TARGETS & PEDAGOGICAL NOTES)

### 1. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 1 (Liking Verbs, Quantifiers & Present Simple)
* **Động từ chỉ sở thích + Danh động từ (Gerund $V-ing$):**
  - $enjoy / adore / prefer / love + V-ing$: *"I really enjoy cooking simple balanced dishes."*
  - $avoid / dislike + V-ing$: *"I try to avoid eating greasy fast food."*
* **Lượng từ & Cụm từ định lượng (Quantifiers & Partitive Nouns):**
  - Món ăn và nguyên liệu thường là danh từ không đếm được, cần dùng cụm từ định lượng:
    - *a steaming bowl of Pho* (một tô phở bốc khói)
    - *a crispy loaf of Banh Mi* (một ổ bánh mì giòn rụm)
    - *a pinch of salt / a spoonful of sugar* (một nhúm muối / một thìa đường)
    - *plenty of fresh vegetables* (nhiều rau xanh tươi mát)
* **Kế hoạch tương lai (*Be going to*):**
  - *"This summer, I am going to ask my mother to teach me..."* (kế hoạch có chủ ý từ trước).

### 2. Trọng tâm Ngữ pháp Phần 2 (Taste Adjectives, Past Narrative & Sequence)
* **Tính từ miêu tả cảm quan và hương vị ẩm thực (Sensory Adjectives):**
  - Vị giác: `savory` (đậm đà, thơm ngon), `pungent` (nồng, hăng), `crispy` (giòn), `tender` (mềm ngọt, không dai), `greasy` (ngấy mỡ), `velvety` (mịn màng).
* **Quá khứ tiếp diễn làm bối cảnh (*While we were enjoying the feast...*):**
  - Dùng để thiết lập khung cảnh bữa ăn ấm cúng: *"While we were enjoying the feast, my grandfather toasted to everyone's health."*
* **Cấu trúc so sánh hơn (Comparatives):**
  - *"Home-cooked food is undeniably healthier and much cheaper than restaurant food."*
  - *"It was much cozier than dining in any luxury hotel."*

### 3. Lỗi thường gặp & Cách khắc phục (Common Pitfalls & Anti-Trap Advice)
1. **Lỗi đếm danh từ không đếm được:** Học sinh hay nói *"I eat two breads for breakfast"* $\to$ Chuẩn ngữ pháp phải là *"I eat two slices of bread"* hoặc *"a loaf of bread / a Banh Mi"*.
2. **Lỗi phát âm tên món ăn thuần Việt:** Khi nói tiếng Anh, học sinh có thể giữ nguyên tên tiếng Việt (*Pho, Banh Chung, Bun Dau Mam Tom*), nhưng bắt buộc phải có câu phụ giải thích ngắn gọn bằng tiếng Anh: *"which is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup with beef"*.
3. **Lỗi dùng "delicious" quá nhiều:** Thay vì lặp lại *very delicious*, hãy dùng các từ thay thế sinh động như *savory, flavorful, mouth-watering, appetizing*.

## HƯỚNG DẪN CÁ NHÂN HÓA CHO GIÁO VIÊN (TEACHER PERSONALIZATION & SCAFFOLDING)

### 1. Đơn giản hóa câu hỏi cho học sinh yếu (Simplifying & Break-down Questions)
- *Nếu học sinh ấp úng khi được hỏi về thành phần món ăn:*
  - *Thay vì hỏi:* "Could you describe the culinary ingredients and preparation method of your favorite dish?"
  - *Chẻ nhỏ thành:* "What is inside your favorite food? Does it have beef, pork, or vegetables? Is it salty or sweet?"
- *Nếu học sinh không biết nấu ăn:*
  - *Thay vì ép học sinh nói về công thức phức tạp:* Cho phép học sinh nói về món đơn giản nhất: *"I can boil eggs and cook instant noodles with vegetables."*

### 2. Mẫu câu mớm lời an toàn (Safe-Harbor Sentence Starters)
- Giới thiệu món ăn yêu thích: *"When it comes to Vietnamese cuisine, my top choice is definitely..."*
- Nói về bữa sáng: *"On typical mornings, my go-to breakfast is..."*
- Kể chuyện bữa ăn (Part 2): *"I would like to tell you about an unforgettable meal that I shared with my [family/friends] on [occasion]..."*
- Miêu tả hương vị lần đầu ăn: *"At first, I felt a bit hesitant because of the look/smell, but once I tasted it, it was surprisingly..."*
